## Experiment

EXP-41 falsified fixed-linear look-ahead at **alpha=1.0** (full catch-up: theta_hat=2*theta[t-20]-theta[t-40]):
alignment lifted (+0.027) but training collapsed via length-explosion (val@100 0.0478 vs 5/5 band 0.7066).
Diagnosis: the full-catch-up projection made the anchor too fresh/sharp; the signed_ema merger over-amplified it.

**Hypothesis:** with **delay_K = cadence = 20 held fixed**, a SHORTER projection horizon (alpha<1) yields a
gentler, less-sharp anchor that survives 100 steps without collapse and reaches cell A's 5/5 band.

The projection distance is decoupled from staleness via the NEW `lookahead_strength` (alpha) knob:
`theta_hat = (1+alpha)*theta[t-20] - alpha*theta[t-40]` — alpha = fraction of K projected forward.
