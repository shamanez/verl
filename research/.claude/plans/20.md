## Hypothesis
At equal logical PP byte budget (PowerSGD `rank=102` carries `n·102` coordinate-values per token-layer, identical to the PRF mask at `p=0.95`, since `q·H = 0.05·2048 = 102.4`), both arms running vanilla GRPO on GSM8K with `clean_cadence=5` (a dense full-gradient "clean" step every 5th step — 40 compressed + 10 dense steps over a 50-step horizon) and with anchor + spectral OFF, the PowerSGD-compressed reward trajectory **tracks or beats the byte-matched PRF-mask baseline**. Numeric thresholds (all read from `runs/EXP-20/metrics/*.jsonl`):
- `reward_mean@step50 (PowerSGD r=102) >= reward_mean@step50 (PRF mask p=0.95)` (the byte-matched bar; tolerance −0.02 absolute reward, i.e. PowerSGD may sit at most 0.02 below the mask and still "track").
- mean per-step `|Δreward|` between PowerSGD and the mask over the 50 steps `<=` the mask run's own mean gap between its compressed steps and its adjacent clean steps (PowerSGD must not be more jagged relative to dense refresh than the mask is).
- mean dense-vs-compressed **update cosine** (PowerSGD) `>=` mean dense-vs-compressed update cosine (mask) − 0.05 (direction agreement at least as good as the mask, within tolerance).
- `powersgd_q_cond` finite at every logged step (no basis collapse); `powersgd_reconstruction_rel_error` bounded `< 1.0` at every step (codec is doing better than discarding the signal).
- zero NaN/Inf in any logged loss/grad field; zero OOM; all 4–8 GPUs live for the whole run.

**Falsified** (→ STOP) if PowerSGD `reward_mean@step50` underperforms the byte-matched mask by more than the 0.02 tolerance at equal budget, OR PowerSGD destabilises (NaN/Inf, `q_cond` non-finite, reconstruction error ≥ 1.0, or a GPU-stall/OOM that cannot be fixed on the `exp/*` branch). Dense (`ce_dense_50s_gsm8k`) is an optional ceiling, not the bar.

## Correctness invariants (pre-run gate — REQUIRED, `code_change: true`)

PowerSGD activation compression is new code. Before spending the 50-step sweep, the runner/analyst verify these cheap, machine-checkable invariants in a 1–2 step probe (a ≤2-step run with the same launcher + tiny token budget). A `hard`-gate failure aborts before the sweep (see `## Analyst predicate`). These map directly to the issue's `correctness_invariants` field and Part XI.3 pre-flight checklist.

```yaml
invariants:
  - name:  off-path parity
    check: "comm_eff.enabled=false (or compression_type=dense) ⇒ the train/old-logprob forward is byte-identical to the dense path; logged loss at step 1 equals the dense control's loss at step 1 to fp tolerance (1e-6 rel)"
    gate:  hard
  - name:  r=H lossless limiting case
    check: "with rank = H (full-rank orthonormal Q), M_hat = M Q Qᵀ = M exactly ⇒ powersgd_reconstruction_rel_error <= 1e-4 at every probe step (INF-18 continuity check)"
    gate:  hard
  - name:  autograd / no-STE gradient check
    check: "backward matches the analytic self-adjoint projector dL/dM = (dL/dM_hat)·QQᵀ (finite-diff on a small M, or assert the projected-gradient identity to 1e-3 rel); Q is detached, M stays in-graph, V is accumulated under no_grad OFF the graph (INF-9, Part III.7)"
    gate:  hard
  - name:  determinism / multi-rank Q agreement
    check: "same seed_L (= base_seed·1_000_003 + layer_idx·7919) ⇒ identical t=0 basis Q on every rank, and identical Q after one update on every rank under the parallelism actually used (INF-13, Part III.4)"
    gate:  hard
  - name:  Q frozen across the paired GRPO forwards
    check: "within one global step the old-logprob recompute and the actor-train forward see the SAME Q_t; Q updates only AFTER the gradient-bearing actor-train work (Part V.3). Probe: at step 0 with no weight change, the GRPO importance ratio ρ = exp(logπ_new − logπ_old) ≈ 1 (INF-17 — a spurious ρ≠1 means the two forwards used different bases)"
    gate:  hard
  - name:  backend integration (FSDP + grad-ckpt + dtype, no NaN/OOM)
    check: "the boundary hook fires inside FSDP-wrapped transformer blocks without breaking flat-param/no_sync/summon_full_params; QR/orth runs in fp32 while the projection runs in the activation dtype (INF-14); under gradient checkpointing the recomputed boundary forward sees the identical frozen Q; the ≤2-step probe completes with no NaN/Inf in M_hat, Q singular values, or grad_norm, no OOM, and powersgd_q_cond finite"
    gate:  hard
```

The point is to fail fast and cheap on a broken implementation rather than pay for a 50-step sweep that was never interpretable. New code almost always surfaces backend-integration bugs on first launch (FSDP sharding / activation-checkpointing recompute / bf16-QR blow-up / autograd / OOM). When that happens it is mandatory to iteratively diagnose and FIX it — edit on the `exp/<ID>-<slug>` branch → re-run the ≤2-step probe → repeat (the commit-hotfix loop) — until every `hard` invariant passes. Do not stop at the first error. Likely failure surfaces are named in `## Notes for runner`. Emit `STUCK:` and halt only as a genuine last resort, when a fix needs a design decision or an upstream change.

Register the whole block with defaults from issue VII.1.
- **Branch from `origin/vast-ai-workload`, never `main`.** Push `exp/20-<slug>` to origin BEFORE training launches so the branch survives a laptop crash. The protect-upstream hook only permits verl/ writes on `exp/*` (and `vast-ai-workload`).
- **Most likely failure surface = FSDP + activation checkpointing.** Per the EXP-16 anchor-clone FSDP naming bug (memory): nested-FSDP module names drop the `._fsdp_wrapped_module` infix under deepcopy/fallback, so a boundary hook keyed by module name can silently key the wrong thing. Verify the boundary hook fires inside the FSDP-wrapped transformer blocks without breaking flat-param / no_sync / summon_full_params. Under gradient checkpointing the boundary forward is RECOMPUTED — the frozen-per-step Q rule (Part V.3) must guarantee the recompute sees the IDENTICAL Q (this is also why determinism is a hard invariant).
- **fp32 QR, activation-dtype projection (INF-14):** orthonormalize Q in fp32 at init and at every `orth(V)` update; store/apply Q in the activation dtype for the forward. bf16-QR loses orthogonality (`QᵀQ` drifts from I), degrades the projector identity `P²=P`, and inflates reconstruction error + bias. This is a frequent NaN/q_cond source — fix it in the probe, not the sweep.
- **Autograd (INF-9, Part III.7):** forward `M_hat = (M @ Q) @ Q.T` keeps M in-graph with Q detached, so backward is the same self-adjoint projector `dL/dM = (dL/dM_hat)·QQᵀ` — NO straight-through estimator. The basis-update statistic `V += M.detach().float().T @ (M.detach().float() @ Q)` is accumulated under `torch.no_grad()`, OFF the graph. Accumulate V ONLY on compressed train forwards (not on old-logprob recompute), then `orth(V)` once at end-of-actor-update when `not clean_step and global_step % update_cadence == 0`; clear V.
- **The single GRPO-required change (Part V.3):** Q is FROZEN for the entire global step — old-logprob recompute uses `Q_t`, actor-train forward uses `Q_t`, the update to `Q_{t+1}` happens only AFTER the gradient-bearing actor-train work, next global step uses `Q_{t+1}`. Do NOT update Q during old-logprob recompute. This is what makes the GRPO importance ratio ρ≈1 at epoch 0 with no weight change (INF-17), the frozen-Q hard invariant.
