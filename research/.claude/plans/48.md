## Hypothesis
Fixed second-order (curvature) projection of ANCHOR weights improves **out-of-sample** projection accuracy over the #47 best fixed rule (OOS damped-linear), specifically in the short-horizon region h ≤ 10. Concretely, second-order is carried forward only if, on the current fast subsampled CORE-4 harness over the EXP-57 fp32 trace, the best fixed second-order arm (`naive_second_order` or `damped_second_order` with an OOS-selected curvature scale) BEATS the regenerated #47 damped-linear baseline at the primary per-step operating point Δ=10,h=10 by a material margin — median `weight_proj_ratio` lower by ≥ 0.01 absolute (i.e. `ratio_2nd_op ≤ ratio_damped_lin_op − 0.01`) OR `h_safe` strictly greater (more global steps with GLOBAL median ratio < 1.0) — while NOT regressing the global operating-point ratio above hold-stale (ratio < 1.0). **Falsification (the kill rule):** if second-order fails to lower the operating-point ratio by ≥ 0.01 AND fails to extend h_safe versus the regenerated #47 damped-linear cell, second order buys no real OOS gain and #56 keeps the simpler linear rule (VERDICT = STOP / do-not-carry-forward — a scientifically valid negative result, still a completed lane).
