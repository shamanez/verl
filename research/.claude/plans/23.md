## Hypothesis

A `delay_K=5` **stale** full-gradient re-anchor — full GRPO forward/backward from
weights theta_{t-5}, EMA'd into `M_anchor`, combined with the live PowerSGD-compressed
gradient via `spectral.correction_mode=inject` (delayed error feedback) or `=blend`
(convex combine toward the stale full gradient), with the fresh clean step OFF
(`clean_cadence=0`) — recovers most of the fresh-clean benefit for PowerSGD r=77.
Falsifiable thresholds (judged on the GSM8K full-test val curve `val-core/openai/gsm8k/acc/mean@1`
at step 50, corroborated by the train-reward trajectory `critic/score/mean`):
**(Success)** `max(val@50(A2), val@50(A3)) >= 0.7315` (within ~1.0 pt of A0's
0.7415) **AND** `>= val@50(A1) + 0.05` (clearly above the no-refresh floor). If the
A1 floor itself lands within 0.01 of A0 (i.e. PowerSGD needs no refresh at all),
that is a stronger PASS of the parent direction and is recorded as such.
**(Falsified)** `max(val@50(A2), val@50(A3)) <= val@50(A1) + 0.02` (stale re-anchor
indistinguishable from no refresh) — staleness breaks the re-anchor; emit STOP with
the next-lever readout (error-feedback codec vs smaller delay_K).

## Correctness invariants (pre-run gate — REQUIRED, code_change=true)

The anchor (`comm_eff.anchor`) + spectral-correction (`comm_eff.spectral`) circuit
has **only ever been exercised with `compression_type=prf_mask`** (EXP-16/17/18/21);
it has **never run with `compression_type=powersgd`**. The grad-correction hook
(`FSDPEngine._maybe_comm_eff_grad_correction`, transformer_impl.py:1246) is
codec-agnostic by construction — it reads `g_mask = p.grad` AFTER FSDP reduction and
BEFORE clip, regardless of which codec produced that grad — so it *should* compose,
but "should" is not "does". Additionally the launcher does NOT currently expose
`spectral.correction_mode` / `inject_gamma` / `blend_eta` (grep rc=1), so an
un-patched run would silently fall to the dataclass default `correction_mode="reweight"`,
which prior project evidence (memory: EXP-21) proved **INERT** (cos~0.5, G_filt≈0) —
NOT the `inject`/`blend` the arms require. Both are fixed on the `exp/*` branch and
verified by a ≤2-step on-box smoke BEFORE the A1-A3 arms launch.
