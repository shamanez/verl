## Hypothesis

The step-1 `grad_norm` explosion (~1134, ~3000x the dense reference of 0.36) and the ~50-step entropy collapse (6.4 -> 0.023) observed at paper scale (`TRAIN_BATCH=128, ROLLOUT_N=8, MAX_PROMPT=1024, MAX_RESPONSE=16384`) live in the **comm-eff method**, not in the disabled-path scaffolding, and the **accumulation-timescale** component is fixable by a periodic clean (unmasked) optimizer step alone — without the anchor/spectral apparatus. Falsifiable predictions, each machine-checkable: **(Gate, Test 1)** with comm-eff OFF at baseline batch knobs, step-1 `actor/grad_norm <= 0.46`, `actor/entropy` within 0.10 of 0.37, `critic/score/mean` within 0.05 of 0.12, AND scaffold-noop Cell B matches Cell A within the same tolerances (Cell B step-1 `grad_norm <= 1.0`); if Cell B's `grad_norm > 1.0` the scaffolding regressed the backend (corrective code-change trigger). **(Peel, Test 2, diagnosis-only)** pure masked GRPO (`mask.enabled=true, p=0.9, anchor.enabled=false, spectral.enabled=false`) is expected to exhibit a step-1 `actor/grad_norm > 10` and/or an entropy decline over 10 steps, with no FSDP/DTensor/NaN error (any such error is a STOP for that cell). **(Fix, Test 3, mandatory)** with `clean_cadence=10`, Cell B keeps `actor/grad_norm` bounded (every masked-step `grad_norm <= 0.5x` the no-clean Cell A `grad_norm` at the same step for steps 40-100) and holds `actor/entropy >= 1.0` through step 60, while Cell A (`clean_cadence=0`) drifts toward collapse (`entropy < 0.5` by step 60), and Cell B completes >=60 (target 100) steps with **zero** FSDP/DTensor/Ray-unhandled/NaN/OOM errors. **(Audit, Test 4, conditional)** only if Test 2 was stable while the full method explodes, or Test 3's clean step failed: distinguish whether the spectral blend amplifies (B explodes, C `<<` B at step 1) versus the anchor harvest / FSDP integration (B ~= C, both explode), localising to a named module path. FSDP-no-errors is a HARD acceptance criterion for **every** cell.
