## Hypothesis
With masking enabled (`comm_eff.mask.p=0.95`), a two-step GRPO smoke that also
performs at least one validation pass (`TEST_FREQ=1`, `val_before_train=True`)
and at least one checkpoint save (`SAVE_FREQ=1`) shows: (1) the per-path mask
counter is strictly 0 on rollout generation, old log-prob recompute, reference
log-prob, validation/eval generation, checkpoint save, checkpoint load, and
`infer_batch`, and strictly > 0 only inside actor-train forward/backward;
(2) `old_log_prob` and `ref_log_prob` recomputed with masking enabled equal
those computed with masking disabled on the same sequences within relative
tolerance 1e-6; (3) validation `val/test_score` is identical (within scoring
noise) whether masking is on or off; (4) a checkpoint saved with masking
enabled contains no comm_eff/mask tensors and reloads to bit-identical actor
weights. Falsified if any non-train path shows a nonzero mask count, old/ref
log-probs differ between mask-on and mask-off beyond 1e-6, validation output is
perturbed by mask config, or any comm_eff/mask state leaks into a checkpoint or
into synced rollout weights.

## Notes for analyst
- Mask confinement is the single most important falsifier. Extract by metric KEY PREFIX, not substring: EXP-5's verdict documented that the naive grep `comm_eff/mask_applications.*(rollout|log_prob|ref|val|infer|checkpoint)` yields FALSE POSITIVES, because metrics are emitted as one physical line per step that also carries `timing_s/old_log_prob`, `timing_s/ref`, etc. downstream of the mask counter. The correct check confirms the only mask-applications key is `actor/comm_eff/mask_applications`; any rollout/log_prob/ref/val/infer/checkpoint-KEYED mask counter is the falsifier.
- Metrics may arrive as `train.log` rather than `metrics/*.jsonl` (as in EXP-5). `analyze.py` keys off the (possibly absent) jsonl and emits a non-authoritative scaffold; evaluate the headline criteria via the explicit greps above against `train.log`. `check_budget.py` is authoritative for spend/caps.
- The 1e-6 log-prob equality and path-isolation unit-test outcomes come from the codex-verify `verify.log`, not the train log. PASS requires both the live-smoke evidence and the codex-verify assertions.
- `diff_against_baseline.py --baseline baseline` is used here only as the dense-path regression check on the mask-off reference cell (per the EXP-5 plan precedent), not as a performance comparison. p95/staleness/communication metrics are n/a for this contamination-guard issue.
- p90/p95 grad_norm magnitude is not a criterion — finiteness is. A high-but-finite grad_norm in a 2-step smoke is not a falsifier.
