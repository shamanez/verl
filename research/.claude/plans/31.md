## Hypothesis

On the LOCKED comm-eff substrate (Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, GSM8K, vanilla GRPO
no-KL/no-entropy; PowerSGD **r=77 act-basis** as the base codec, anchor **owns Q**
via power iteration, **cadence=delay_K=5**, **clean_cadence=0**, batch 128 / mini
64 / lr 1e-6 / n=8 / resp 16384 / seed 0, **replay_paired_batch=true**), the
B2 δ-residual merger (`G_corr = G_comp + λ·δ`, λ=1, β_anc=0) reconstructs the
*dense* gradient and therefore **caps at parity** (honest prior on a greedy
surpass ≈ 10%, almost entirely from one route). The falsifiable claims, in
cheap-first order:

1. **(zero-GPU, gating) Basis-mismatch is real and a δ-routed gradient sub-basis is sized correctly.** Re-reading the stored EXP-30 artifacts, the valid replay gradient has stable-rank `1.8–2.05` and top-1% mass `0.58–0.61` (m7), and the δ residual concentrates: a rank-`r_δ∈{16,8}` truncation of the stored per-fire δ retains **≥ 95%** of δ's Frobenius energy, AND the act-basis Q's principal angle to the replay-gradient top-2 subspace is **large** (cos(G_true, span(Q_act)) consistent with the F1 `≈0` / EXP-26 off-principal `0.682`). If the rank-16 truncation retains < 95% OR Q already spans the gradient top-2 (principal angle ≈ 0), the sub-basis route is *killed for free* before any GPU spend.
2. **(headline, greedy-surpass) A `tail`/`grad` gradient sub-basis (rank 2–4) routed ADDITIVELY into the K-delayed, fire-refreshed, β_anc=0 correction term only — leaving forward Q = act (r=77) UNCHANGED — lifts the seed-replicated comm-eff val band ABOVE the dense band on greedy mean@1.** Numeric: best `val@50 ≥ 0.79` (strictly above the same-config dense 0.7839) AND seed-replicated mean exceeds the dense mean by `> pooled-SE (≈0.020)`, emission-free through ≥ 100 steps (no `response_length/max > 4000` in any post-warmup step). **FALSIFIED** if val does not exceed the dense band beyond pooled-SE, OR the sub-basis ignites (a length-explosion STOP).
3. **(secondary, GOAL-3 savings-surpass) δ compressed to `r_δ∈{16,8}` holds the B2 val band (within pooled-SE) at a strictly-lower HONEST amortized inter-stage byte cost than B2's standing ~4×** (honest = anchor-M all-reduce + Q-broadcast counted, NOT the fast-path 19.8×). Not a val-surpass (truncation error is biased, expected val effect ≤ 0) — a legitimate "better than dense (which trains at 1×)" on the savings axis.
4. **(secondary, pass@k coverage edge) Under pass@k (k∈{4,8}) or temperature-n sampling, the comm-eff band EXCEEDS the dense band by > pooled-SE under the IDENTICAL eval AND the advantage GROWS with k** (compression-specific, not generic). Reported explicitly as "surpasses under pass@k (coverage edge), parity under greedy" — never as a greedy surpass. Carries a mandatory dense×{T,n} control.
5. **(closing certification) B2 ×3 + dense ×3 seed replicates** pin both bands so every "above-B2 / above-dense" claim above is a difference of two pinned distributions, not a single-draw point. Decision bands: `|mean_B2 − mean_dense| ≤ 0.020` ⇒ PARITY ESTABLISHED; `mean_dense − mean_B2 > 0.040` ⇒ DEFICIT.

The headline target is claim 2 (greedy val > dense). Claims 3 and 4 are credited
as additional win conditions (GOAL-3 + pass@k), and claim 5 certifies whatever
wins. The hypothesis is falsified for the headline if, after the sub-basis cell
runs to ≥100 steps under the seeded protocol, the comm-eff band does not clear
the dense band beyond pooled-SE.

### The analysis that shapes every cell (operator's "demonstrate you understood what failed and why")

1. **The codec error is ~92% of the compressed-gradient energy and ⊥ the true gradient (F1).** At identical (batch, θ): `cos(δ, G_comp_ring) ≈ −0.92…−0.98`, `‖δ‖/‖G_comp_ring‖ ≈ 1.05`, forcing `cos(G_anc_rep, G_comp_ring) ≈ 0` with `‖G_anc_rep‖ ≈ 0.33·‖G_comp_ring‖`. B2 works because `δ = G_anc_rep − G_comp_ring` simultaneously **cancels** the stale codec artifact AND **re-injects** the true direction. A *blend* (B1, 0.7422) can only add a near-orthogonal partner — geometrically dominated. **Implication:** the merger axis (signed_ema/blend/inject/λ-grids) is exhausted; the lever is *what directions the codec/correction carries*, not the merge dose.

2. **The gradient is stable-rank ≈ 2 but the act-basis Q misses it (m7 + F1).** `‖G‖²_F/‖G‖²₂ ≈ 1.8–2.05` (vs ambient 1536), top-1% mass ≈ 0.60. A rank-77 codec has *abundant* capacity for a stable-rank-2 object — the failure is **basis MISMATCH** (act-basis Q does not span the gradient's principal directions; EXP-26 measured the act-basis captures only ~0.318 of update energy, off-principal share 0.682). **This is the single most promising lever for surpassing dense: inject the rank-~2 true direction the act-basis structurally misses.**

3. **A faithful reconstruction of dense cannot beat dense (feasibility §3c).** B2's ceiling IS the dense gradient (it reconstructs dense-on-stale-data); the ext100 late decay 0.7536@50→0.7400@100 is the staleness drift expressing B2 sitting *below* dense at horizon. **To surpass you must inject signal dense LACKS.** The retracted "shorter delay_K" was a parity-defense for that drift (now BLOCKED). The surviving on-axis surpass route is a sub-basis that injects the true direction *more cleanly than B2's drift-limited telescoping* — and (the honest deflation, feasibility §5) even that route's realistic ceiling is *clean parity* unless an unproven compression-specific conversion effect materializes on the greedy bar (rated < 20%). Hence P(greedy surpass) ≈ 10%, and this plan also banks the GOAL-3 + pass@k wins.

4. **The EXP-26-Step-C corner is the binding exclusion (DEAD route, do NOT re-enter).** Building/blending Q from gradient energy as the *forward* basis anti-converts — because rollouts are uncompressed, a gradient-tuned forward Q breaks the activation reconstruction the codec must serve (recon plateaued at 0.68). **The sub-basis cell avoids this BY CONSTRUCTION: it routes the gradient directions into the correction term δ ONLY; the forward/recon Q stays act, r=77.** The existing `tail`/`grad` family-sketch machinery (`_compute_family_V`/`_build_family_Q`/`_family_probe`, validated as the EXP-26 Step-C1 *passive* screen in `test_q_family_screen_exp26.py`) is reused as the sub-basis *source* — it already harvests gradient directions passively without touching forward Q.

5. **The carrier law gates anything injected every step (EXP-25/27 STOP, F2/F4).** A *persistent exogenous* direction added to the brakeless GRPO update rectifies into a length-explosion (token-mean ratchet ~86× tail amplification). The discriminator is carrier *presence*, not dose (damping only delays ~20 steps). B2 survives because δ is *endogenous* (cancels the circuit's own artifact). **A static sub-basis injected every step is exactly the exogenous carrier the law convicts** — so the sub-basis MUST be delivered K-delayed / fire-refreshed / β_anc=0, inheriting B2's m6≈0.62 base persistence (τ/cadence≈2, marginal) PLUS a fresh ignition surface. Hence the P1/P2/P3 + m6<0.85 trip-wires are live on every training cell, and the sub-basis cell runs ≥100 steps (the censoring horizon).

### Vast.ai utilization discipline
- **One box for the whole sequence.** Step 0 is zero-GPU (laptop). A → D → C chain
  back-to-back on ONE provisioned box (shared docker/verl/dataset cache); pack a
  D run and a C run as concurrent tmux sessions partitioning the 4 GPUs only if
  both gates are OPEN and memory headroom allows (D at 100 steps has ~zero
  headroom — prefer sequential). Cell E is eval-only (short, runs on the same box
  while a train cell finishes or on a fresh short box). Cell F packs 2 runs/box.
- **Tear down the instant the science is captured** — the analyst reads metrics
  from the laptop `runs/EXP-31/`, never the box. No keep-alive.
- **Idle-gap note for the monitor:** Cell E (eval-only) and the F seed packing
  have brief weight-load / vLLM-init gaps; otherwise no known-idle phase — the
  GPU-stall watchdog's default thresholds apply (see ## Notes for runner).

The docker image / shm / onstart / disk / CUDA filter live in the locked Vast
Template (`templates.json`) — this plan names no `template_hash` or `image`.

## Success criteria
- [ ] (code_change) every `hard`-gate box in ## Correctness invariants passes the pre-run probe — gates the sweep. In particular: **off-path parity** (feature OFF == B2 bitwise) and **Step-C avoidance** (forward Q checksum == act-only run; recon_rel_error in the act band, NOT the 0.68 plateau) are green.
- [ ] every launched training cell reaches its `>= step_target` (50 for A/C/F; 100 for D) without NaN / non-finite gradients / OOM.
- [ ] (comparison) controlled variables hold equal + ASSERTED across arms: `bytes_ratio(D) == bytes_ratio(B2)` within [0.0500,0.0510]; `forward_Q_checksum(D) == forward_Q_checksum(act-only)` each step; β_anc=0, λ=1, codec seeds, batch/mini/lr/n/resp identical (resolved_params diff vs B2 is exactly the intended knobs).
- [ ] Cell A reproduces the B2 band: `val@50 ∈ [0.7528 − 0.024, 0.7528 + 0.024]`, ZERO post-warmup emission through 50.
- [ ] **HEADLINE (Cell D, greedy-surpass):** best `val@50 ≥ 0.79` AND seed-replicated `mean_D − mean_dense > 0.020` (pooled-SE), emission-free through ≥ 100 steps (no `response_length/max > 4000` in any post-warmup step; no P1/P2/P3; m6 stays < 0.85). [If GATE-SUBBASIS CLOSED at Step 0, this box is marked `n/a — sub-basis route killed for free; see Step-0 result` and the headline reverts to certified parity + the secondary wins.]
- [ ] (Cell C, GOAL-3 savings-surpass) at r_δ∈{16,8}: `val band ≥ B2 band` (within pooled-SE 0.020) AND honest amortized inter-stage ratio STRICTLY below B2's ~4× (Step-0 denominator recomputed with compressed δ).
- [ ] (Cell E, secondary pass@k) comm-eff `pass@k band > dense pass@k band` by > pooled-SE under identical sampling AND the advantage GROWS with k; reported as "pass@k coverage edge, greedy parity".
- [ ] (Cell F, certification) both bands pinned: B2 ×3 + dense ×3 @50; `|mean_B2 − mean_dense| ≤ 0.020` (PARITY) or `mean_dense − mean_B2 > 0.040` (DEFICIT) decided; the winning improvement cell's 3-seed mean is the certified surpass/parity number; no B2 seed ignited.

(val `val-core/openai/gsm8k/acc/mean@1`; emission `response_length/max`; bytes
`comm_eff/bytes_ratio`; Q checksums + recon from the powersgd lines) for the

## Analyst predicate
- **PASS** iff every box in ## Success criteria is checked. **Two PASS tiers are
  reportable and BOTH are wins:** (i) **GREEDY-SURPASS PASS** — Cell D clears the
  headline box (val ≥ 0.79, mean > dense by > pooled-SE, emission-free ≥100);
  (ii) **PARITY+SAVINGS+COVERAGE PASS** — Cell F establishes parity AND (Cell C
  GOAL-3 savings-surpass OR Cell E pass@k coverage edge) holds, even if Cell D
  did not clear the greedy bar (this is the *expected* outcome at ~10% greedy-
  surpass prior, and is a complete GOAL-2/3 win). State which tier in the verdict.
- **REVISE** if at most `iterations` boxes are unchecked AND the analyst can name a
  concrete next-action knob for each. Output `next_actions:` as yaml
  `{knob, from, to, rationale}` objects (e.g. `{knob: cell_D_subbasis_rank, from: 2, to: 4, rationale: "rank-2 sub-basis under-captured the gradient top-2; m7 stable-rank up to 2.05"}` or `{knob: cell_D_subbasis_family, from: tail, to: grad, rationale: "act-deflation cost too much energy; raw grad sub-basis"}`). NEVER REVISE onto a BLOCKED route (§Rescue / §BLOCKED).
- **STOP** if the headline is falsified (Cell D below the dense band beyond pooled-SE at ≥100 steps with no nameable non-blocked knob) AND no secondary win holds, OR an ignition STOP fires, OR budget exhausted, OR `iterations` REVISE cycles consumed. **A greedy-surpass STOP with parity+savings+coverage holding is reported as the PARITY+SAVINGS+COVERAGE PASS tier, not a STOP** (the honest ~10% prior means a greedy-surpass miss is the modal result, and the GOAL is satisfied by parity).
- **Pre-run gate**: any `hard`-gate failure in ## Correctness invariants is an
  automatic STOP for that cell (fix the code in a new code_change cycle). In
  particular a Step-C-avoidance failure (forward Q changed / recon plateaued) is
  an immediate STOP — the sub-basis leaked into the forward path.
