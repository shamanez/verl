## Hypothesis

On the LOCKED comm-eff substrate (Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, GSM8K, vanilla GRPO
no-KL/no-entropy; **PowerSGD r=77 act-basis** base codec, anchor **owns Q** via
power iteration, **cadence=delay_K=5**, **clean_cadence=0**, batch 128 / mini 64 /
lr 1e-6 / n=8 / resp 16384 / seed 0, **replay_paired_batch=true**), the way the
**stale anchor gradient** is folded into the fast compressed update is the only
thing that decides whether we beat dense.

The B2 δ-residual merger (`G_corr = G_comp + λ·δ`, λ=1, β_anc=0) uses the stale
anchor gradient to reconstruct the *dense* gradient on stale data — so it **caps
at parity** (val@50 0.7528, and it decays past 50: 0.7536@50 → 0.7400@100, the
staleness drift of sitting just below dense). To SURPASS dense you must extract
from the stale anchor gradient the **true-gradient signal the act-basis
structurally misses** and fold it in more cleanly than B2's drift-limited
telescoping.

Falsifiable claims:

1. **(HEADLINE — greedy surpass) A rank-~2 "off-principal" direction harvested
   from the STALE ANCHOR GRADIENT and routed ADDITIVELY into the K-delayed,
   fire-refreshed, β_anc=0 correction term — forward Q left UNCHANGED at act
   r=77 — lifts greedy val ABOVE the dense control.** Numeric: best
   `val@50 ≥ 0.79` (strictly above the same-config dense 0.7839) AND the
   seed-replicated mean exceeds the dense mean by `> pooled-SE (≈0.020)`.
   **FALSIFIED** if, after the merger cell runs to its step budget under the
   seeded protocol, the comm-eff band does not clear the dense band beyond
   pooled-SE.

2. **(SECONDARY — savings, optional) The stale-anchor correction `δ` can be
   transmitted in `r_δ ∈ {16,8}` columns at held val**, beating the standing
   honest ~4× amortized inter-stage byte cost. A legitimate "better than dense
   (which trains at 1×)" on the savings axis. Pursued only if it does not delay
   the headline and the box is free.

3. **(CLOSING — certification) B2 ×3 + dense ×3 seed replicates** pin both bands
   so every "above-dense" claim is a difference of two pinned distributions, not
   a single draw. Decision bands: `mean_cell − mean_dense > pooled-SE (≈0.020)`
   ⇒ SURPASS established; `|mean − mean_dense| ≤ 0.020` ⇒ PARITY.

The headline is claim 1. The hypothesis is falsified for the headline if the
merger cell, run to its step budget at seeds, does not clear the dense band.

### The analysis that shapes the merger (operator's "demonstrate you understood what failed and why")

1. **The codec error is ~92% of the compressed-gradient energy and ⊥ the true
   gradient (F1).** At identical (batch, θ): `cos(δ, G_comp_ring) ≈ −0.92…−0.98`,
   `‖δ‖/‖G_comp_ring‖ ≈ 1.05`, forcing `cos(G_anc_rep, G_comp_ring) ≈ 0` with
   `‖G_anc_rep‖ ≈ 0.33·‖G_comp_ring‖`. B2 works because the stale-anchor residual
   `δ = G_anc_rep − G_comp_ring` simultaneously **cancels** the stale codec
   artifact AND **re-injects** the true direction. A *blend* (B1, 0.7422) can only
   add a near-orthogonal partner — geometrically dominated. **Implication:** the
   merge *dose* is exhausted; the lever is *what directions the stale-anchor
   correction carries*.

2. **The gradient is stable-rank ≈ 2 but the act-basis Q misses it (m7 + F1).**
   `‖G‖²_F/‖G‖²₂ ≈ 1.8–2.05` (vs ambient 1536), top-1% mass ≈ 0.60. A rank-77
   codec has *abundant* capacity for a stable-rank-2 object — the failure is
   **basis MISMATCH**: the act-basis Q does not span the gradient's principal
   directions (EXP-26 measured the act-basis captures only ~0.318 of update
   energy; off-principal share 0.682). **This is the single most promising lever
   for surpassing dense: harvest the rank-~2 true direction the act-basis
   structurally misses FROM THE STALE ANCHOR GRADIENT, and add it to the
   correction.**

3. **A faithful reconstruction of dense cannot beat dense (feasibility §3c).**
   B2's ceiling IS the dense gradient (it reconstructs dense-on-stale-data); the
   late decay 0.7536@50→0.7400@100 is staleness drift expressing B2 sitting
   *below* dense at horizon. **To surpass you must inject signal dense LACKS / use
   the stale anchor more cleanly than raw telescoping.** The surviving on-axis
   route is a stale-anchor sub-basis that injects the true direction more cleanly
   than B2's drift-limited residual.

4. **The EXP-26 Step-C corner is the binding exclusion (DEAD route, do NOT
   re-enter).** Building/blending Q from gradient energy as the *forward* basis
   anti-converts — because rollouts are uncompressed, a gradient-tuned forward Q
   breaks the activation reconstruction the codec must serve (recon plateaued at
   0.68). **The merger cell avoids this BY CONSTRUCTION: it routes the stale-anchor
   gradient directions into the correction term `δ` ONLY; the forward/recon Q stays
   act, r=77.** The existing `tail`/`grad` family-sketch machinery
   (`_compute_family_V`/`_build_family_Q`/`_family_probe`, validated as the EXP-26
   Step-C1 *passive* screen in `test_q_family_screen_exp26.py`) is reused as the
   sub-basis *source* — it already harvests gradient directions passively without
   touching forward Q.
