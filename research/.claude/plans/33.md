## Hypothesis

On the corrected SOTA comm-eff substrate (B2 = `delayed_ef`, λ=1.0, valid-M PowerSGD r=77
anchor circuit, `replay_paired_batch=true`), the anchor-gradient EMA decay `β_anc` is the
only knob never swept under control, and the standing design hypothesis is that **freshness
beats averaging** in comm-eff GRPO — i.e. **β=0 (no averaging, latest fire's `M_rep`) is
optimal** and any `β>0` weakly-or-strictly degrades greedy GSM8K val@50. β is purely
training-side (it does not change communication bytes), so all five cells share the SOTA's
`bytes_ratio` and the sweep isolates the staleness effect at a fixed comm budget. Falsifiable
numeric reads, all single-draw seed 0 on the same provisioned box (rollout noise ±0.024):
(a) **Control reproduces** — C0 (β=0) lands in the B2 band `val@50 ∈ [0.716, 0.774]`
(B2 reference 0.7354–0.7528; ±0.024), confirming the sweep's β=0 endpoint is the SOTA.
(b) **Degenerate bracket** — C4 (β=1) collapses to the plain-PowerSGD no-merger floor
`val@50 = 0.6300 ± 0.024` (i.e. `∈ [0.606, 0.654]`): with β=1 the EMA `M_rep = β·M_rep`
stays at its `torch.zeros` cold-start forever, the cold-M guard makes `delayed_ef` a strict
no-op, and the cell degrades to plain PowerSGD-with-Q-updated (= the corrected no-merger
floor, WandB `k6nmcuyd` = 0.6300). This is the staleness→∞ endpoint, not a genuine
averaging point.
(c) **β=0 is (weakly) optimal — the headline curve.** The science is the shape of C1–C3
(β=0.25/0.5/0.75) between the C0 control and the C4 floor. The standing hypothesis predicts
**no `β>0` cell strictly beats C0 beyond +0.024**: `max(val@50[C1,C2,C3]) − val@50[C0] <=
+0.024`. The hypothesis is **falsified** (freshness is NOT strictly best, some averaging is
free or helpful) iff any of C1/C2/C3 lands `val@50 >= val@50[C0] + 0.024` (a genuine
beyond-noise improvement over the β=0 SOTA) — the only way this sweep returns a non-trivial
positive. Either outcome (graceful monotonic decline supporting freshness-best, a flat
free-averaging region, a cliff, or a surprise β>0 winner) is a recordable FINDING; the
deliverable is the β→accuracy curve with the β=0-vs-best-β>0 gap reported against ±0.024.
