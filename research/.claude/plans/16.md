## ⚠ PLAN AMENDMENT (2026-05-30) — no-rescale REMOVED; every comm-eff cell uses rescale

**Finding (now CLOSED): no-rescale masking blows up the gradient, and we know exactly why.**
Masking ~90% of the residual-stream activations **without** the inverted-dropout
`1/(1-p)` rescale collapses the activation RMS; the downstream **pre-RMSNorm backward
scales as `1/RMS`**, so the collapse amplifies every gradient crossing the norm and
compounds over the 7 boundaries. Measured on this exact run (Qwen2.5-1.5B / GSM8K / GRPO,
4×B200):

| config | grad_norm | × dense |
|---|---|---|
| dense (comm-eff OFF) | **0.38** | 1× |
| mask p=0.9 **+ rescale** | **~4.5** | ~12× (bounded variance cost) |
| mask p=0.9 **no-rescale** | **~2700** | ~7100× (RMS-collapse blow-up) |

Root cause is fully documented in **`notes/grad_norm_blowup_norescale_rmsnorm.md`**
(triangulated across single-GPU bf16, full-model CPU fp32, elementary autograd, and a
`p`-sweep): it is **NOT** FSDP, **NOT** gradient checkpointing, **NOT** a mask/IS
inconsistency, **NOT** a bug. It is RMSNorm's `1/RMS` backward × the no-rescale RMS
collapse, and it is **architecture-general to any pre-norm transformer** (Llama 3.2,
Mistral, Gemma, Qwen2.5, and GPT-2/LayerNorm via `1/std`). **rescale is the fix**
(removes the bias/RMS-collapse; the residual ~12× is the bounded `p/(1-p)` variance cost
that anchor + spectral + lower-`p` exist to reduce).

**DECISION — applies to this experiment and ALL future ones:** no-rescale is **excluded**.
The former no-rescale cells (old **cell 1** and old **cell 3**) are **REMOVED** — re-running
them only re-confirms a closed finding and burns compute. **Every comm-eff cell from here
uses `MASK_RESCALE=true`.** The comparison anchor is no longer a no-rescale cell; it is the
**dense reference** (final cell).

## Hypothesis

Within the first 10–25 trainer steps, at least one communication-efficient
configuration (mask+rescale, mask+clean-cadence, or mask+rescale+spectral+anchor)
can stay **numerically stable** — finite `grad_norm` throughout, **zero** NaN/Inf
in any loss/grad/reward field, no FSDP backward/hook error, no DTensor↔Tensor
writeback error — AND move **measurably closer to the dense GRPO learning
trajectory** than the current unstable high-mask control (mask-only, no-rescale,
`p=0.9`). Falsifiable thresholds (a PASS requires ALL): (a) the dense reference
cell 6 itself stays finite and shows a non-negative `train/reward_mean` /
`val/test_score` trend over its 25 steps (the screen's own sanity anchor); (b) at
least one comm-eff cell reaches its full `TOTAL_TRAINING_STEPS` with `grad_norm`
finite at every logged step and **max `grad_norm` within 10×** the dense cell's
max `grad_norm` (same order of magnitude); (c) that cell's GRPO clipping is
**not saturated** — `actor/pg_clipfrac` does not climb monotonically toward 1.0
and `actor/pg_clipfrac_lower` is not pinned (a non-saturated, non-degenerate
clipping signature); (d) that cell's step-20/25 `train/reward_mean` (or
`val/test_score` where logged) is **strictly greater** than the mask-only
no-rescale control's value at the same step. If NO comm-eff cell clears (b)+(c)+(d),
the high-mask method is judged not-yet-stabilizable by these knobs at `p=0.9` on
this screen and the verdict is STOP/REVISE per `## Analyst predicate`. These are
operational stability + relative-trajectory thresholds appropriate to a short
diagnostic screen — they do not assert dense parity (that is the later 25-step
follow-up's job under `## Follow-up gate`).

RE-CONFIRMS it on the actual provisioned GPU and adds an end-to-end IS-ratio
check, for BOTH rescale settings.

**What is asserted (and what is NOT).** The mask is a deterministic integer PRF
(`prf_token_mask`, splitmix64) keyed ONLY on
`(base_seed, layer_idx, global_step, sample_id, position_id, channel)` — it has
NO `forward_call_id` / `ppo_minibatch_id` / `microbatch_id` / `fsdp_rank` /
`packed_offset` term. So the keep/zero **mask pattern** is **bit-identical**
(exact — there is no floating point in the mask itself) across:
- **(a) the `old_log_prob` recompute vs the train forward** at the same `global_step`;
- **(b) every PPO minibatch and PPO-epoch forward INSIDE one `global_step`** — the
  per-prompt mask does NOT change as we loop over minibatches;
- **(c) gradient-checkpoint forward-recompute**;

and the mask is **independent per pipeline boundary** (`layer_idx` is in the key →
each pipe boundary draws its own pattern) but **consistent across the passes** at
each boundary.

**Do NOT assert bit-identity on the masked product `h*mask`.** The mask pattern
is exact, but `h*mask` (and `h*mask/(1-p)` under rescale) can differ by **tiny
floating-point noise** when the upstream activations `h` are not bit-reproducible
across the two differently-packed forwards (different kernels/packing). So:
compare the **`{0,1}` mask tensor exactly**, and compare `h*mask` (if at all)
only **within an FP tolerance** — or feed the **same** `h` into both passes, in
which case `h*mask` is exact too.

**Test steps — run for BOTH `COMM_EFF_MASK_RESCALE=false` and `=true` (p=0.9, mask_recompute=true):**
1. **Reuse the existing test-lock on the box:**
   `pytest tests/workers/comm_eff/test_activation_mask.py -v`
   (`test_cross_packing_consistency`, `..._through_hook`,
   `test_different_step_different_mask`, `test_boundary_indices_*`,
   `test_register_installs_hooks_on_boundaries_only`) — these assert "same token →
   same mask under any packing at a fixed step", "different step → different mask",
   and "masks only on boundary layers".
2. **Cross-pass mask equality:** drive `prf_token_mask` / `ActivationMasker` twice
   with the SAME `global_step` + SAME per-token `(sample_id, position_id)` and
   assert the `{0,1}` mask is bit-identical at every boundary (zero mismatch).
3. **End-to-end IS ratio ≈ 1 pre-update:** before any optimizer step (first inner
   PPO minibatch), with `mask_recompute=true` the old_log_prob and the current
   log_prob run through the SAME masked subnetwork on the SAME weights, so
   `exp(log_prob − old_log_prob)` must be **≈ 1 within FP tolerance**. A ratio that
   is NOT ≈ 1 pre-update means the two forwards saw different masks (or weights) —
   a corrupt importance ratio. (This is the practical, end-to-end sibling of the
   mask-equality assertion.)
