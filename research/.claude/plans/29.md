## Problem statement (confirmed at code level)

The anchor refresh (`verl/workers/engine/fsdp/transformer_impl.py:1237`, invoked at the top of
every `train_batch` mini-batch tick from `verl/workers/engine/base.py:192`) loads the tick-`t−K`
weight snapshot into a clone but forwards **`anchor_data = data.copy()` — the CURRENT tick's
batch** (`transformer_impl.py:1429`, `:1557`). `anchor_pg_loss` (`verl/workers/comm_eff/anchor.py:116-204`)
is ratio≡1 REINFORCE (`-advantages·logπ`, `old_log_probs` deliberately ignored), and the
advantages come from rewards of the CURRENT policy's rollouts (vLLM holds θ_{t−1} when generating
step t's batch). So `G_anchor = ∇θ[-A·logπ_θ]` at θ_{t−K} on samples from π_current with no
importance correction — neither the stale policy's true gradient nor the current one's. For the
decentralized-PP target, a stale worker computes gradients on trajectories generated from ITS OWN
weights. This was a deliberate design ("clean step's gradient at stale weights"), not a bug, but
it is a standing confound in M's content across EXP-25/26/27.

**Fix (easy path, operator-blessed):** ring-buffer past mini-batches ("tokens + their advantages")
paired with the weights that generated them; at fire time replay `(batch[t−K], gen_snapshot)`.
No re-generation, no rollout-path changes.

**Key alignment fact:** a snapshot taken at the FIRST `train_batch` tick of global step G (before
any optimizer tick of G) is exactly the weights vLLM used to generate step G's rollouts. One
snapshot per global step + per-tick batches ⇒ exact `(batch, generator-weights)` pairs, warmup
included. Realized weight staleness becomes K or K+1 ticks (logged); data staleness = K ticks;
weights ≡ generator always.

**Verified enablers** (read-only audit 2026-06-12):
- The batch TensorDict is already CPU-resident at `train_batch` time (only per-micro-batch
  `.to(get_device_id())` in `forward_step`, `transformer_impl.py:3024`) ⇒ batch ring costs ~0 HBM.
- NJT (jagged) clone/device-moves are the existing production hot path; torch pinned 2.9.1.
- Deep clone at store time REQUIRED: `_forward_backward_batch_inner` mutates `data` in place via
  `tu.assign_non_tensor` (`transformer_impl.py:961-969`).
- `_forward_backward_batch_inner` re-derives `batch_num_tokens`/`dp_size` from the batch it is
  given ⇒ replayed batch renormalizes correctly; the all_reduce stays rank-symmetric.
- `engine._comm_eff_global_step` is stamped before the mini-batch loop
  (`engine_workers.py:802-811`) ⇒ robust gs-boundary detection; `_maybe_comm_eff_anchor_refresh`
  has exactly one call site.
- TODAY the legacy snapshot ring is GPU-resident (`device=None` at `transformer_impl.py:1370`):
  `delay_K+1` ≈ 6 full bf16 snapshots ≈ ~18.6 GB HBM. Moving it to CPU is numerics-neutral (clone
  load casts back via `s.to(p.device, p.dtype)` at `:1493`).

## Hypothesis
With `anchor.replay_paired_batch=true` + `anchor.snapshot_device=cpu` on the locked EXP-27
substrate (powersgd r77 q_basis=act sync_basis, anchor owns_q cadence=5 delay_K=5, clean=0,
ef_powersgd ef_clip=0.5 ef_decay=0.5), a 25-step smoke (a) passes every correctness invariant
(value-level canary equality, exact pairing post-warmup, zero mask/optimizer leakage), (b) shows
`perf/max_memory_allocated_gb` strictly BELOW EXP-27's healthy-phase 57.9 GB (the GPU snapshot
ring is gone) with step time within +1.5 s of EXP-27, and (c) reaches val@25 ≥ 0.60 with
`comm/bytes_ratio` ≈ 0.0505 unchanged. (val@25 is a not-broken gate, not a parity gate — replay
changes the science; refs: EXP-27 val@25 0.7134, EXP-26 B_ef_r2 val@25 0.674.)

  actor max_token_len 18432 with anchor on.
- Diagnostics policy (FIXED_CONTROL_SURFACE §Diagnostics): capture machinery stays OFF; the canary
  is scalar-only and always-on by design.
- Replay-mode semantic shifts to NOT mistake for bugs: (a) owns-Q sketch V now harvests
  STALE-batch activations at stale weights (watch `reconstruction_rel_error`/`q_cond` — drift is
  expected, divergence is not); (b) `capture_fresh_anchor` probe semantics change in replay mode
  (dump-only, capture is OFF here anyway); (c) realized weight staleness alternates K / K+1 ticks
  by construction (logged as `realized_step_delay`).
- Monitor actively from first launch (watch-logs-for-early-failures): Traceback/OOM/NaN grep +
