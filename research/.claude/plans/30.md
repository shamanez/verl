## The math + pre-registered decision rules (carried verbatim from issue #30)

```
Fast circuit (every tick):    G_comp(t) = ∇_θ L_clip(batch_t; θ_t)   under compressed boundaries
Old anchor feed (falsified):  G_anc_old(t) = ∇_θ [−A·logπ_θ](batch_t; θ_{t−K})        generator MISMATCH
EXP-29 replay feed (valid):   G_anc_rep(t) = ∇_θ [−A·logπ_θ](batch_{t−K}; θ_{t−K})    generator MATCH (ratio≡1 exact)
Old merge partner (retired):  M_old = β·M + (1−β)·G_anc_old,  β=0.95   (~50-step memory + invalid estimate)
New merge partner (tested):   M_rep = G_anc_rep(latest fire),  β_anc=0.0  (5-tick hold, refreshed each fire)

Cell B1 (blend, exists at spectral_filter.py:255):
  G_corr = (1−η)·G_comp + η·(‖G_comp‖/‖M_rep‖)·M_rep
  ‖G_corr‖² = ‖G_comp‖²·[(1−η)² + η² + 2η(1−η)·c],  c = cos(G_comp, M_rep)   ← Step-A gated

Cell B2 (delayed exact residual, new mode):
  δ(t)   = G_anc_rep(t) − G_comp_ring(t−K)        # codec error on IDENTICAL (batch, θ)
  G_corr(t) = G_comp(t) + λ·δ(t)
  Σ_t G_corr ≈ Σ_t G_full(t−K) + drift terms       # bounded-lag recovery; λ=1, REVISE knob λ

Carrier-law compatibility (why this does not relitigate #27):
  ignition needs ‖Σ_t e_t‖ ~ λ·T·‖G‖ ⇒ carrier autocorrelation time ≫ cadence.
  M_rep refreshes every fire; its cross-fire autocorrelation cos(M_rep(t), M_rep(t−5)) is
  LOGGED in Step A — if it is high, persistence risk returns and the verdict must say so.
```

### The sharp falsification questions (verbatim)

- **Q1 — Was the orthogonality an artifact of the invalid M (H_validity) or of batch
  decorrelation (H_decorr)?** Decided by the Step-A triple at each fire:
  `cos(G_comp, G_anc_rep)` vs `cos(G_comp, G_anc_old)` vs `cos(G_anc_rep, G_anc_old)`, plus
  the fast gradient's own lag-autocorrelation `cos(G_comp(t), G_comp(t−j))`, j=1..5.
  H_validity ⇒ replay-cos ≫ old-cos. H_decorr ⇒ both ≈0 with the M-pair cos high and
  lag-autocorrelation ≈0 at j≥4 — in which case **no K-delayed signal of any quality can
  help, the merger class is dead on ALL M variants, and #28 (current-step residual, the only
  design surviving this null) proceeds with raised confidence.**
- **Q2 — If geometry opens, does a valid, short-memory, direction-preserving-by-magnitude
  merge convert into val without emission?** Decided by Cell B1 (blend, η=0.3, β_anc=0) at
  50 steps under the standing emission monitors.
- **Q3 — Is the codec's weight-gradient error recoverable by a K-delayed exact residual?**
  Step A directly measures the within-pair codec error `δ(t) = G_anc_rep(t) −
  G_comp_ring(t−K)` on identical (batch, θ) (the weight-space re-measure of EXP-26's
  activation-proxy 0.318, mod clip-vs-ratio≡1 mismatch which the relevance probe bounds at
  ~0.01 nats). Cell B2 (conditional) applies `G_corr(t) = G_comp(t) + λ·δ(t)` — the
  K-delayed analogue of #28's telescoping EF, needing only a fire-aware fast-grad ring (the
  c512128 retention pattern), no codec code.

### 1. Stay open on the combination operator — B1/B2 are two points, not the space

The object under study is the **combination operator** `combine(G_comp, anchor signal) →
G_corr` — how an anchor gradient (including EMA-accumulated anchor gradients, not solely
the β_anc=0 latest-fire form) is merged with the PowerSGD-style compressed fast gradient.
The design space is wider than the two pre-registered instances:

- **convex blends** (B1 is one point: η=0.3, magnitude-matched);
- **residual / error-feedback corrections** (B2 is one point: K-delayed exact residual, λ=1);
- **projection-based splits** (route the anchor signal only into the codec's null space /
  off-principal directions);
- **magnitude-vs-direction decompositions** (trust one source for direction, the other for
  scale);
- **EMA-smoothed variants at intermediate β_anc** (0 < β_anc ≪ 0.95) — variance reduction
  on the valid feed without the retired long-memory carrier.

Read the experiment as: **Step A characterizes the geometry; B1/B2 are the first two points
sampled from this space; the m1–m7 table is the map for choosing further points if they
miss.** Concretely:

- **m1** (the `c` in the B1 norm identity) prices every convex blend at once — the measured
  cosine determines the whole η-curve, which is why no pre-gate η sweep exists;
- **m5** (`‖δ‖/‖G_comp‖`, `cos(δ, G_comp_ring)`) prices residual-style operators: whether
  the codec error is large enough to matter, bounded enough to inject, and how it is
  oriented;
- **m7** (stable rank, top-1% mass) + **m3** inform projection/decomposition-style
  operators — whether the valid anchor gradient is concentrated enough for a split to be
  meaningful;
- **m4** bounds ALL K-delayed operators uniformly: if the fast gradient decorrelates with
  itself by j≈4–5 ticks, no delayed signal of any form survives transport (H_decorr);
- **m6 is the explicit safety measurement for the EMA-smoothed variants**: the carrier law
  convicts persistence (autocorrelation time ≫ cadence), so the cross-fire
  `cos(M_rep(t), M_rep(t−5 ticks))` is exactly the number that says whether a small-β_anc
  EMA could be safe in a successor. m6 LOW ⇒ a short-effective-memory EMA plausibly stays
  below the ignition scale and is a legitimate follow-on proposal; m6 HIGH ⇒ persistence
  risk is intrinsic to the valid anchor signal itself, even β_anc=0 carriers inherit it,
  and the verdict must say so (already required in §The math).

Hard boundary, restated so the openness cannot be misread: **inside EXP-30**, training
cells run β_anc=0 only (the β=0.95 long-memory carrier stays retired per the carrier law),
the gates stay exactly as pre-registered, and no training arm beyond B1/B2 (+ the single
permitted REVISE re-roll) may be added in flight. The openness clause licenses follow-on
DESIGN — it sharpens what gets proposed after EXP-30 — not in-run scope growth.

### 2. Theoretical behavior primer — what we know, and what Step A measures

**(a) How GRPO/RLVR gradients behave.** GRPO's gradient is a high-variance policy-gradient
estimate (group-normalized advantages over n=8 rollouts, REINFORCE-shaped): per-batch noise
can dominate the shared signal component. Two consequences are load-bearing here. First,
the batch-to-batch decorrelation risk — **H_decorr**: if the signal fraction is small at
batch 128, gradients on different batches are near-orthogonal REGARDLESS of estimator
validity, and **m4** measures this directly on the fast circuit itself (same estimator,
same circuit; only the batch and ≤5 ticks of weight drift differ) — the clean null for the
whole K-delayed program. Second, the on-record RLVR-geometry facts that motivated this
issue: RLVR weight updates are NOT concentrated in the activation principal subspace
(act-basis update-energy capture only **0.318**, off-principal **0.68** — EXP-26 Step A);
merger-vs-fast sign agreement is structurally **~coin-flip** at delay 0 (what killed the
SL-validated signed_ema in RLVR — EXP-25); and the external team's "RLVR gradients are
sparse / differently-distributed than SFT" premise has a never-measured half — **m7**
(stable rank `‖G‖_F²/‖G‖₂²`, top-1% coordinate mass) finally measures it on a
textbook-valid PG gradient. The validity axis itself — **H_validity** — is isolated by the
paired **m1-vs-m2** comparison within one run, with **m3** closing the triangle: every
prior orthogonality reading (cos ≈ 0.001–0.06) was taken only on the generator-mismatched,
50-step-EMA M.

**(b) How PowerSGD-compressed gradients behave.** The codec projects boundary traffic onto
a rank-77 basis, so the compression error is a projection residual — **biased, not
zero-mean** (EXP-25: the error is bias-dominated at ~**42:1 SNR**; the dropped energy is
small but systematically oriented). A biased per-step error does not average out — it
accumulates as a drift the optimizer cannot see. The textbook fix for biased compression is
**error feedback** — re-inject the residual so the telescoping sum recovers full
information — and the zero-mean alternative (mask+rescale) is already on record as trading
the bias for variance and stalling. #28 builds the current-step EF form on the codec's own
dropped residual. In THIS architecture, however, full passes exist only in the anchor and
`delay_K ≥ 5` staleness is mandatory (realism non-negotiables), so the anchor-feasible
analogue is the **K-delayed exact residual**: `δ(t) = G_anc_rep(t) − G_comp_ring(t−K)` on
identical (batch, θ) — B2's telescoping `Σ G_corr ≈ Σ G_full(t−K) + drift` (§The math).
**m5** decides whether δ behaves the way a recoverable codec residual must: identifiable
(loss-mismatch ≤ ~0.02 nats ⇒ it is codec error, not objective mismatch), bounded
(`‖δ‖/‖G_comp‖` median ∈ [0.1, 1.5] — big enough to matter, small enough to inject), with
`cos(δ, G_comp_ring)` reporting its orientation. m5 is simultaneously the weight-space
re-measure of EXP-26's activation-proxy 0.318 — the direct prior for #28's primitive.

**(c) What "stability" means in RLVR here.** The failure mode of every harmful merger in
this program is the length-explosion spiral, and 50-step survival is a CENSORED statistic
(EXP-27: the damped-EF arm ignited at step ~61, beyond the old horizon; α0.5 was already
pinning at 47–48). So stability is judged by **EMISSION, not survival** — zero post-warmup
`len/max > 4000` and no P1, per the watch authority — not by finishing the run. The
mechanism is the **carrier law** (#27 post-mortem; verbatim in §The math): ignition needs a
persistent fixed-direction force, `‖Σ_t e_t‖ ~ λ·T·‖G‖`, which requires the carrier's
autocorrelation time ≫ cadence; the β=0.95 EMA (~50-step memory) supplies exactly that —
the law convicts persistence + exogeneity, not anchor information per se. That conviction
leaves a 2×2 carrier matrix, {long, short memory} × {invalid, valid gradient}, with three
cells settled: long×invalid ran and either ignited (signed_ema, ef) or sat inert at
cos≈0.001 (blend/inject); long×valid is excluded (the retired carrier — persistence
convicts regardless of validity); short×invalid was never run and is moot now that
validity is free (EXP-29). **Short-memory × valid is the one never-run cell — B1/B2 are
exactly that cell**, run under the standing emission monitors. **m6** is the in-run check
that the "short-memory" label is actually TRUE of M_rep (low cross-fire autocorrelation)
rather than assumed.

Theory → measurement map (every claim above lands on a Step-A number):

| theory point | Step-A metric | reading |
|---|---|---|
| validity hypothesis H_validity | m1 vs m2 (paired) + m3 | m1 ≫ m2 ⇒ generator match moves the geometry (GATE-B1) |
| batch-decorrelation hypothesis H_decorr | m4 (j=1..5), with m1≈m2≈0 + m3 high | m4 ≈ 0 at j≥4 ⇒ no K-delayed signal of any quality helps |
| biased codec residual, EF-recoverable | m5 ratio + cos + loss-mismatch | median ∈ [0.1, 1.5] AND ≤ ~0.02 nats ⇒ δ identifiable (GATE-B2) |
| carrier-law persistence risk | m6 | HIGH ⇒ persistence returns even at β_anc=0; also the safety bound for any future small-β_anc EMA variant |
| RLVR sparsity/rank premise (team) | m7 | first measurement on a valid PG gradient; priors for #28 + successor combination operators |
