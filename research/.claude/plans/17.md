## Hypothesis
Comm-eff masked GRPO (`p=0.9`, rescale ON, `mask_recompute=true`, per-(token,channel) mask at the 7 pipeline boundaries `[3,7,11,15,18,21,24]` of Qwen2.5-1.5B) with the optimizer stepping on the **true unmasked dense gradient every 20 steps** (`clean_cadence=20`, anchor+spectral OFF), run for 2 epochs (~116 steps) on GSM8K, **keeps learning and the periodic clean step keeps fully repairing the weights** (no irreparable drift). Concretely, falsifiable thresholds: (1) `val/test_score_final >= val/test_score_step0 + 0.05`; (2) `actor/grad_norm` finite throughout — masked-step median `< 10`, clean-step (20/40/60/80/100) `~0.4`; (3) clean-step `grad_norm` does **not** trend upward across the 5 clean steps (each clean step still fully re-anchors); (4) clean-step (true-policy) `actor/entropy` neither collapses toward 0 nor climbs toward the masked-forward ~5.9 while reward declines, and clean-step `val` stays `>> 0`; (5) the train-inference gap is **stationary** — |slope| of pearson(actor,rollout), `rollout_corr/kl`, and `training/rollout_probs_diff_mean` over masked steps is within noise (R^2 < ~0.3 or slope ~ 0), and binning each masked step's gap by steps-since-last-clean (0..19) shows no monotone within-window growth (or any growth fully resets at each clean step — a clean-resettable sawtooth, not a ratchet); (6) masked-step `pg_clipfrac` does not saturate (well below ~0.15; ideally `<= 0.08`); (7) boundary-activation comm-savings ~= 85.5% (19/20 masked at p=0.9). **Falsified if** val diverges / collapses / saturates-then-drops, grad_norm goes non-finite, clean-step (true-policy) entropy collapses toward 0 OR climbs toward the masked value while reward falls, clean-step `grad_norm` trends upward across 20/40/60/80/100 (repair losing ground to drift), or the train-inference gap grows monotonically (a ratchet, not a clean-resettable sawtooth).

## Notes for analyst
- **This is a characterization run, not a parity certification.** PASS requires *learning + stability + stationarity + repair + no-drift-to-random*, NOT dense parity. Do not STOP merely because final val undershoots dense's 0.741 — that is expected (only ~5 true-gradient steps over 116) and is the *point* of the learning-speed diagnostic.
- **Weight by the long-horizon trends, not the absolute level.** The four load-bearing reads, in priority order: (1) **clean-step `grad_norm` trajectory** across 20/40/60/80/100 — flat ⇒ repair (PASS-ward), monotone-rising ⇒ accumulating irreparable drift (STOP-ward). (2) **clean-step (true-policy) `entropy`** across the 5 clean steps — sharp/flat-or-down ⇒ healthy true policy; rising toward the masked-forward ~5.9 while reward falls ⇒ weights degrading toward random (STOP-ward). IGNORE masked-step entropy (~5.9) for policy health — it is a mask-forward artifact, not the weights. (3) **train-inference gap stationarity** — fit slopes on pearson / `rollout_corr/kl` / `rollout_probs_diff_mean` over masked steps AND bin by steps-since-last-clean (0..19): flat-vs-position ⇒ mask-caused-not-drift (PASS-ward); rising-with-position-then-reset-at-clean ⇒ clean-resettable sawtooth (still PASS); monotone-ratchet-across-run ⇒ FAIL. (4) **per-clean-step repair delta** (metric just-after minus just-before each clean step, for val/gap/entropy) — constant/growing ⇒ healthy, shrinking ⇒ damage outpacing repair (STOP-ward).
- **`pg_clipfrac` is the protective-valve proxy.** Stable ~0.04 ⇒ the ratio clip is containing each masked update (on-policy GRPO protects the weights); climbing toward saturation *between* clean steps ⇒ the masked policy is outrunning the K=20 re-anchor — that specific signature is the REVISE->tighten-cadence trigger (and feeds the issue's Open Question 3 on GRPO as a weight-protection mechanism).
- **Learning-speed overlay is a diagnostic the next algorithm consumes**, not a gate: report steps-to-reward>=0.5, final-val gap to dense, and reward slope against EXP-16 cell6 (dense 0.741) / cell7 (clean@5 0.729) / cell4 (clean@4 0.696), all read from runs/EXP-16/metrics. Where possible, attribute the reward rise to masked-windows vs clean steps by reading reward per step (issue Open Question 1).
