## Hypothesis

The EXP-37 collapse appeared "shortly after step 50," which is exactly where GSM8K's first epoch ends
(7473 train examples / 128 batch = **58.4 steps/epoch**; step 50 ≈ 0.86 epoch; epoch-2 boundary ≈ step 58;
100 steps ≈ 1.71 epochs). **Hypothesis under test:** that collapse is driven by the **20/20 anchor latency**,
not by the epoch boundary. The control is the known-good **5/5** latency (the EXP-36B accel base, never run past
50 steps) extended to **100 steps**, everything else identical.

Two interpretable outcomes (this is a disambiguation, not a surpass-dense claim):
1. **5/5 stays stable through 100 steps** (no NaN, no length-ignition / entropy-collapse / cold-M fallback across
   steps 50-100) → the step-~50 collapse is **specific to the 20/20 latency**; the epoch-boundary explanation is
   **rejected**. (Expected outcome.)
2. **5/5 also collapses near the epoch boundary (~step 58)** → the collapse is **epoch / dataset-revisit driven**,
   not the latency → this **reopens** the EXP-37 interpretation and is itself a publishable finding.

Sanity floor (this is the base latency, so step-50 behavior should reproduce EXP-36B): `val@50 >= 0.6862`
(within 0.05 absolute of EXP-36B 0.7362). The decisive observable is back-half (50-100) stability, NOT the val number.

## Analyst predicate
