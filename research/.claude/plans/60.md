## Hypothesis

**HEADLINE MUST (deliverable A): deliver a single ranked OUT-OF-SAMPLE projection-accuracy table** over the CORE-4 ladder + the #49 self-correcting arms + the fixed #47 damped-linear bar, scored on the **Big-Math (EXP-58)** fp32 trace at the primary operating point, and **NAME the single best-fitting projection method** (highest `pred_evr_pooled`). Absolute accuracy — per-window projection R² / explained variance (`pred_evr_pooled`, PRIMARY) and per-scalar prediction-R² (`pred_r2_scalar_*`) — is FIRST-CLASS for EVERY arm including `hold_stale` (the do-nothing baseline); `weight_proj_ratio` (vs `hold_stale`) is the comparative verdict metric. Head-to-head ladder, all on the SAME axes: `hold_stale` · `naive_linear` · `damped_linear` (#47 fixed, `--lam-grid`) · `naive_second_order` · `damped_second_order` (#48 fixed, `--mu-grid`) · `adaptive_linear` · `adaptive_second_order` · the #49 rows-v49.1 arms (`rolling_ls_k`+`--adaptive-lookback`, `ewma_momentum`+`--coef-momentum`, `--adaptive-warmup`/`--adaptive-gain-schedule`).

**CROSS-DATASET question (deliverable B, the falsifiable claim): the GSM8K projector winner generalizes to Big-Math.** Concretely, on the EXP-58 trace at the primary op-point (**per-tick Δ=5, h=10**), the fixed #47 damped-linear rule (a) reproduces its favorable GSM8K behavior — GLOBAL-median `weight_proj_ratio < 1.0` (beats do-nothing) with positive `pred_evr_pooled` — and (b) remains at or near the top of the ranked accuracy table. The #49 self-correcting arms are re-scored on Big-Math and judged by the **SAME prefer-simplicity kill-rule as #49**: an adaptive arm "wins on Big-Math" only if it lowers the GLOBAL-median op-point ratio by **≥ 0.01 absolute** vs the regenerated fixed damped-linear bar **OR** strictly extends `h_safe`, WITHOUT regressing above `hold_stale` (op-point ratio < 1.0), AND shows a positive absolute-accuracy gain (`pred_evr_pooled` delta > 0 vs the fixed bar).

**LONGER-HORIZON showcase.** The EXP-58 trace spans **1000 global training steps** at 20-step spacing (50 snapshots), so `h` ticks ⇒ `h·20` global steps. Beyond the primary op (h=10 = 200 global steps) the lane MUST also report the two long-horizon cells Δ=5,h=20 (400 global steps) and Δ=2,h=30 (600 global steps). Because those cells have FEW windows (see the window-count table), the verdict MUST attach **bootstrap/CI (or p10/p90) to the op-point ratio-diff-vs-bar** so a few-window result is not over-read.

**CROSS-DATASET verdict (the deliverable that FEEDS #56 — do NOT close #56):** state, with numbers, whether the GSM8K winner (fixed damped-linear) **clears on Big-Math** (ratio < 1.0, ranks at/near the top of the accuracy table), and whether **ANY** arm's gain over the fixed bar is **≥ the 0.01 prefer-simplicity threshold** on Big-Math. Two honest terminal outcomes, BOTH a PASS (symmetric falsification, mirroring #48/#49):
- **generalizes** — the GSM8K winner clears on Big-Math too (fixed damped-linear beats do-nothing at the op point and ranks at/near top; no adaptive arm earns ≥0.01 → prefer-simple carries ACROSS datasets), OR a GSM8K-winning adaptive arm also clears on Big-Math.
- **dataset-specific** — the winner does NOT reproduce on Big-Math (fixed damped-linear fails to beat `hold_stale`, OR a different method ranks top, OR a GSM8K-winning adaptive arm no longer clears). A scientifically valid COMPLETED result, reported as such to #56.

Numeric thresholds (mirror #49): op-point GLOBAL-median ratio < 1.0 for the winner; ratio-improvement ≥ 0.01 OR strictly-greater `h_safe` for any "adaptive wins" claim; positive `pred_evr_pooled` delta vs the fixed bar. **Falsification is symmetric** — a clean "dataset-specific" negative is a completed PASS, not a failure. Even under a prefer-simple / dataset-specific outcome, the HEADLINE accuracy ranking (deliverable A) is still emitted and the best-fitting method is named.
