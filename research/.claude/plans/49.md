## Hypothesis

**HEADLINE MUST (the #1 deliverable): find the BEST WAY to FIT and PROJECT future weights = the method with the highest OUT-OF-SAMPLE projection accuracy.** Absolute projection accuracy — per-window projection R² / explained variance (`pred_evr_pooled`), per-scalar prediction-R² (`pred_r2_scalar_*`), and a relative-error norm of `theta_hat_{t+h}` vs the true `theta_{t+h}` — is the PRIMARY, first-class metric for EVERY arm including `hold_stale` (the do-nothing baseline); `weight_proj_ratio` (vs hold_stale) stays as the comparative verdict metric. The lane MUST rank ALL methods by OOS accuracy at the primary op-point and NAME the single best-fitting projection method (a machine-checkable headline, not a footnote). The full head-to-head ladder, all scored on the SAME axes: `hold_stale` (do-nothing) · `naive_linear` (normal linear) · `damped_linear` (#47 fixed, `--lam-grid`) · `naive_second_order` (normal second order) · `damped_second_order` (#48 fixed second order, `--mu-grid`) · `adaptive_linear` (self-correcting linear) · `adaptive_second_order` (self-correcting second order) — PLUS the NEW adaptive/regression variants below. The fixed/naive arms are the FREE #48-cached bar; the adaptive + new variants are the new compute.

**Falsifiable feedback question (the second, gated deliverable):** retrospective feedback from *completed* forecast misses, turned into a small online / regression-fit update of the extrapolation **coefficient** (tiny-state, ANCHOR-friendly), can make projection MATERIALLY more accurate out-of-sample than the fixed #47 damped-linear rule — beyond the harness's existing expanding-window adaptive arm. Concretely, at the primary per-step operating point Δ=10, h=10, at least one NEW adaptive/regression arm (regression LS-fit of the global damping/velocity scalar over completed causal fires — bounded-last-K vs the existing expanding window, SWEEP K; a joint 2-parameter LS regression of velocity+curvature; coefficient EWMA+momentum; a warmup/gain step-size schedule; a trust-region/residual clip; and — ONLY if a single global scalar is proven insufficient — a few coarse per-block_type/super_block scalars; ALL with strict no-peek fitting through `fit_window_positions()` and ≤ a small constant number of scalars of ANCHOR state) satisfies BOTH: (1) it lowers the GLOBAL median `weight_proj_ratio` by **≥ 0.01 absolute** versus the regenerated #47 fixed damped-linear bar `ratio_damped_lin_op` **OR** strictly extends `h_safe` (more global steps with GLOBAL median ratio < 1.0), WITHOUT regressing the op-point ratio above hold-stale (`ratio < 1.0`); AND (2) it shows a real ABSOLUTE-accuracy gain (higher `pred_evr_pooled` and/or per-scalar prediction-R²) versus that fixed bar. Adaptation must be measured across BOTH regimes (per-step Δ=10,h=10 AND per-tick Δ=20,h=20) and Δ∈{5,10,20}×h — so "adaptation under different regimes" is measured, not assumed. **Falsification / kill rule (symmetric, honest):** if, after iterating the ladder, NO new arm clears BOTH conditions at tiny ANCHOR state, feedback buys no material OOS gain and #56 should prefer the simpler fixed #47 damped-linear rule — a scientifically valid COMPLETED NEGATIVE (VERDICT = PASS, `carry_forward = FALSE`), mirroring #48. (Even under the prefer-simple negative, the HEADLINE accuracy ranking is still delivered — the best-fitting method is named regardless.) Numeric thresholds: ratio-improvement ≥ 0.01 OR h_safe strictly greater; op-point ratio < 1.0; positive `pred_evr_pooled` delta vs the fixed bar. Free prior signal: on #48 the EXISTING expanding-window `adaptive_linear` scored 0.9380 vs `damped_linear` 0.9396 — a margin of only **0.0016 < 0.01** — so the bar is genuinely at risk and the new arms must EARN the 0.01 or the negative stands.
