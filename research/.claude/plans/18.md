## Hypothesis
The EXP-17 comm-eff recipe (per-(token,channel) activation mask p=0.9 at the 7 pipeline boundaries, rescale ON, mask_recompute ON, true dense gradient every 20 steps via clean_cadence=20, anchor+spectral OFF, vanilla no-KL no-entropy GRPO on Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct) is STABLE on GSM8K. Question: does it **collapse** on a substantially HARDER math distribution (Big-Math-RL-Verified-filtered, \boxed{} answers, mean solve-rate far below GSM8K)? Falsifiable collapse signatures: actor/grad_norm non-finite or monotone-blowing-up at clean steps; reward/val crashing to ~0 after initial rise (saturate-then-collapse); entropy collapsing to 0 OR climbing toward the masked-forward value while reward falls; pg_clipfrac saturating; clean-step grad_norm trending UP across 20/40/60/80/100/120 (drift outpacing repair).

## Success criteria (collapse test — diagnostic, not a parity gate)
- [ ] Reaches global_step >= 120; no NaN/Inf in loss/grad_norm/reward/log_prob.
- [ ] actor/grad_norm finite throughout; clean-step (20/40/60/80/100/120) grad_norm bounded and NOT monotone-rising.
- [ ] No reward/val collapse: val does not rise-then-crash-to-~0; entropy does not collapse to 0 nor climb toward the masked-forward value while reward falls.
- [ ] pg_clipfrac does not saturate (stays well below ~0.15).
- [ ] Mask confined to actor-train (comm_eff/mask_applications/{rollout,ref_logprob,val,infer,ckpt}==0); anchor+spectral counters ==0; clean steps freeze mask counters.
- [ ] REPORTED (not gated): full reward/val/grad_norm/entropy trajectories; learning-speed vs EXP-17 GSM8K; per-clean-step repair delta; train-inference gap stationarity (pearson/kl/probs_diff slopes + steps-since-clean binning).

## Analyst predicate
- **PASS (no collapse)**: grad_norm finite + clean-step grad_norm bounded/non-rising, no reward/val crash, entropy healthy, pg_clipfrac unsaturated, mask confinement + anchor/spectral-off hold. Low absolute reward/val on this harder dataset is EXPECTED and is NOT a failure. The finding is "the recipe survives a harder distribution."
- **STOP (collapse — a real, loggable negative result)**: grad_norm non-finite / clean-step grad_norm monotone-blowing-up, reward/val saturate-then-crash, entropy collapse or runaway, or pg_clipfrac saturation — i.e. the masked+periodic-clean dynamic does NOT hold on harder data. This reshapes the algorithm (pushes toward a cheaper continuous on-policy-masked correction rather than periodic re-anchoring).
- **REVISE (<=2)**: if it learns but with a worrying-but-recoverable trend, tighten cadence (clean_cadence 20->10) and re-characterize.
