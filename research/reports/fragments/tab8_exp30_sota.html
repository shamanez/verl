EXP-30: The δ-Residual Merger — Communication-Efficient GRPO Reaches Dense Parity

This is the experiment that produced the program's current state of the art. With a trustworthy anchor in hand (EXP-29), EXP-30 asked the sharpest possible version of the question: instead of borrowing the anchor's signs or its off-subspace energy, what if we feed back the exact difference between the clean gradient and the compressed gradient, measured on the very same data? That correction — the “delayed error-feedback” merger, B2 — trains GSM8K to val@50 ≈ 0.75, reaching the dense band at roughly 5% of the gradient-communication cost, with no length-explosion through 100 steps. It is the direct vindication of EXP-27's closing recommendation: rebuild error-feedback on the codec's own dropped residual, and the exogenous carrier that caused ignition disappears.

Diagnose-then-spend, once more — and a gate that closed

EXP-30 reused EXP-26's discipline: a cheap geometry probe decides which expensive merger earns a training run. A 20-step probe (stepA_geometry_probe) measured the anchor's valid, replayed gradient against the live compressed gradient on identical batches, and two pre-registered gates — thresholds fixed in advance — decided what ran next.

RouteGate (verbatim rule)MeasuredOutcome
Blend (B1) anchor–compressed agreement \(m_1 \ge 0.10\) AND \(m_1 \ge 2 m_2\) on ≥80% of fires \(m_1 = 0.012\), paired-frac \(= 0.57\) CLOSED — never launched

The blend gate closing is itself a result. Even with the now-valid anchor, the clean gradient stays essentially orthogonal to the compressed one (agreement \(\approx 0.01\)) — so a linear blend has almost nothing aligned to add, exactly as EXP-25/26 predicted. The gate retired the entire blend family for the cost of a 4-GPU-hour probe instead of a full training arm. The residual gate, by contrast, confirmed two things the residual route needs: \(\delta\) is the right size (norm ratio ~1) and it is genuine codec error, not an objective mismatch (loss-mismatch 0.01 nats, sitting in EXP-29's relevance band — the payoff of the trajectory fix).

The merger: feed back the exact codec residual

EXP-26's error-feedback re-injected the off-subspace projection of the anchor EMA — and because the projection was nearly vacuous, that reduced to injecting the stale EMA almost raw, an exogenous force that integrated into the tangential carrier and ignited (the EXP-27 story). B2 does something cleaner. Using the valid replayed anchor gradient \(G_{\text{anchor}}^{\text{rep}}\) and the compressed gradient \(G_{\text{comp}}\) on the identical (batch, weights) pair, it forms the exact residual and adds it back, at full strength, with no decay buffer and no sign term:

$$ \delta \;=\; G_{\text{anchor}}^{\text{rep}} \;-\; G_{\text{comp}}, \qquad G_{\text{corr}} \;=\; G_{\text{comp}} \;+\; \lambda\,\delta, \quad \lambda = 1,\ \beta_{\text{anc}} = 0. $$

At \(\lambda = 1\) this telescopes to \(G_{\text{corr}} = G_{\text{anchor}}^{\text{rep}}\): the compressed gradient is used as a transport for the clean one, and the codec error cancels exactly. Because \(\delta\) is the circuit's own dropped residual (endogenous), not an external EMA pushed in sideways, there is no persistent tangential force to integrate — which is precisely why it does not ignite. \(\beta_{\text{anc}} = 0\) keeps the anchor signal memoryless across fires (it is held between fires, not compounded), and the \(K\)-delay is the honest staleness of a slow reference.

Why it works: the within-pair geometry (finding F1)

The probe exposed exactly why a residual succeeds where a blend cannot. Measured at identical batch and weights:

QuantityMeasuredReading
\(\cos(\delta,\ G_{\text{comp}})\)−0.92 to −0.98the residual points almost exactly against the compressed gradient
\(\lVert\delta\rVert / \lVert G_{\text{comp}}\rVert\)≈ 1.05the residual is about as large as the gradient itself
\(\Rightarrow\ \cos(G_{\text{anchor}}^{\text{rep}},\ G_{\text{comp}})\)≈ 0algebraically forced: the true gradient is nearly orthogonal to the compressed one
\(\Rightarrow\ \lVert G_{\text{anchor}}^{\text{rep}}\rVert / \lVert G_{\text{comp}}\rVert\)≈ 0.33the compressed gradient is ~3× too big and mostly off-direction

In plain terms: the rank-77 codec does not gently perturb the weight gradient — the codec error is the dominant component of the compressed gradient. It is three times too large and points almost entirely the wrong way. (This is the weight-space confirmation of EXP-26's activation-space finding that the basis captured only ~1/3 of the update energy.) A blend can only add a near-orthogonal partner and dilute; the residual \(\delta\) simultaneously cancels the stale codec artifact and injects the true direction. That is the whole mechanism.

Two things make B2 work, and the decomposition separates them cleanly. The power-iteration \(Q\)-update is the dominant lever (+~0.5): freeze the basis (C3) and the codec passes noise, validation never leaves baseline. On top of that working substrate, the \(\delta\)-residual merger adds +0.123 (C2 0.6300 → B2 0.7528) — and it edges out the blend (B1 0.7422) by about a point, the residual-vs-blend contrast the gates predicted.

Stability: de-censored to 100 steps

EXP-27 taught the program that a clean 50-step run can be a censored observation of an ignition that fires at step ~61. So B2 was extended to 100 steps (seed 0, identical settings). It traversed the entire EXP-27 ignition band (steps 51–66) cleanly — no length-explosion, no entropy collapse, response-length flat. Validation: 0.7278@25 / 0.7536@50 / 0.7475@75 / 0.7400@100, a mild late decay that still sits well above the realistic floor. Two isolated single-rollout length pins (steps 94, 99) reverted immediately — the benign stochastic-outlier signature, not the ignition fingerprint (which is a climbing mean with collapsing entropy). The per-fire residual ratio settled to ~1.0 and declined all run — bounded, not compounding.

The honest caveat that EXP-31 then resolved. Measured against the same-code, same-config dense rerun EXP-30 added (val@50 0.7839), B2's 0.7528 is −0.031, about 96% of dense — which EXP-30 recorded as near-parity, explicitly not established, pending seed replicates of both arms. Two further findings tempered the claim: the valid anchor signal still carries moderate cross-fire persistence (autocorrelation ~0.62, so \(\beta_{\text{anc}}=0\) stops the carrier compounding but does not make it memoryless — F2), and the replay gradients are extremely low-rank (stable rank ~2, top-1% of coordinates holding ~60% of the mass — F3), meaning a rank-77 codec has ample capacity and the remaining failure is basis mismatch, not capacity. EXP-31 took the binding next step — an apples-to-apples dense rerun on the exact same box — and reframed the bar; see the next tab.
Bottom line. B2 (delayed_ef, \(\lambda=1\)) is the program's SOTA communication-efficient method: GSM8K val@50 in the 0.74–0.75 band, the boundary-activation traffic cut ~19.8× on the fast path (~4× once the anchor's own traffic is honestly amortized in), emission-free through 100 steps for seed 0.
