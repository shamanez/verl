# REPORT — Why masked+clean@20 GRPO matches dense on GSM8K but stalls on Big-Math

**comm-eff-theory team** (synthesizer: theorist). 2026-06-01. Self-contained; supporting inputs folded in.

This is the single self-contained answer to the operator's three questions: (1) What policy is the masked actor following, vs dense? (2) Why does a ~20M× train-inference perplexity mismatch still learn (GSM8K)? (3) Why does it work on GSM8K but stall on Big-Math?

Tags: **[FACT]** provable from code/logs/standard theory; **[INFERENCE]** follows from FACT + stated assumption; **[SPECULATION]** plausible, not established. Papers cited by `literature.md` arXiv id. Derivations in `theory.md`; per-number verification in `empirical_check.md`.

## 1. Executive summary

**Headline.** The activation mask does NOT create a "masked policy" we train or deploy. It creates a biased, high-variance stochastic **ESTIMATOR of the true GRPO gradient** on the ordinary unmasked policy — and we descend it. The deployed policy is always the unmasked weights (validation runs them unmasked). So the whole story reduces to: does the update direction still point uphill on the real objective? On GSM8K yes; on Big-Math no — a signal-to-noise argument, not a broken mechanism.

**Q1 — what policy?** Dense and masked optimize the SAME object: the unmasked policy `π_θ`. The difference is only how the gradient is computed. The masked forward injects per-(token,channel) Bernoulli noise (drop p=0.9, rescale 1/(1-p)=10) at 7 boundaries. Because the GRPO ratio is masked-old vs masked-new (both masked, `mask_recompute=true`), the noise cancels in the ratio (`r_t≈1`, `pg_clipfrac≈0.035` **[FACT]**). The mask corrupts only the gradient that flows back, not the ratio and not the deployed weights. **[FACT/INFERENCE]**

**Q2 — why does a 20M× ppl gap still learn?** That gap is the wrong number. `ppl_ratio≈2.5e7`, `pearson≈0.004`, `kl≈17` all compare the masked training forward to the vLLM **SAMPLER** — a diagnostic that never enters the loss **[FACT]**. The loss only needs the masked gradient to have positive projection onto the true gradient (`⟨∇J, E[g_mask]⟩ > 0`); Adam scale-invariance + GRPO ratio-clip convert that into stable ascent. The clean step every 20 runs the exact dense gradient: it injects a guaranteed-ascent direction AND re-anchors before the mask's systematic bias accumulates enough to flip the projection negative. Result: a **clean-resettable sawtooth** (resets fully at every clean step, 5/5 and 4/4 across the two runs) — the fingerprint of periodic error-correction, not a divergence ratchet. **[FACT for sawtooth; INFERENCE for mechanism]**

**Q3 — GSM8K parity, Big-Math stall.** Decisive control: the clean step repairs IDENTICALLY on both tasks (`kl→0.0004`, `pearson→0.9995`, `ppl_ratio→1.0003` **[FACT]**). So the stall is NOT a repair-machinery failure. It is a signal-to-noise collapse, settled by the base-capability eval:

| dataset | base acc (no RL) | masked+clean@20 final | dense final | reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **GSM8K** | **0.715** | 0.735 (EXP-17) | 0.741 (EXP-16) | +0.02 headroom = **ELICITATION** |
| **Big-Math** | **0.480** | 0.55 flat (EXP-19) | 0.61 (EXP-20) | dense finds +0.06; masked stalls = **gradient-fidelity limit** |

GSM8K is already the model's dominant mode (71.5% zero-RL); RL only sharpens+formats it, which a coarse gradient does easily. Big-Math needs genuine learning of capability the base lacks (48%); the true gradient is small+sparse (dense gains only +0.06, slowly) while the mask's bias+noise floor is task-independent and fixed. So the SNR ratio `‖g_true‖²/(‖b‖²+Var ξ)` collapses on Big-Math. Crucially **dense PROVES the headroom exists (0.61)** — the stall is a lossy-gradient limit, not a missing ceiling. **[FACT table; INFERENCE mechanism]**

Most direct confirmation: on GSM8K reward RISES within masked windows (window 2, steps 21–39: +0.258, slope +0.0118/step, R²=0.85); on Big-Math within-window reward is FLAT (mean slope +0.0004/step, R²≈0) — exactly the positive-projection-vs-zero-projection asymmetry predicted. **[FACT]**

## 2. Theory (condensed from theory.md)

**2.1 Three distributions [FACT].** `π_θ` (deployed, unmasked; val always runs this, `mask_applications/val=0`); `π̃_θ` (masked training forward, `h̃=h⊙m/(1-p)` at boundaries [3,7,11,15,18,21,24]; a different distribution, used only to make a gradient); `μ` (vLLM sampler; feeds `rollout_corr` diagnostics only). GRPO surrogate `L=−E[A_t·clip(r_t,1-ε,1+ε)]`, `r_t=exp(log π̃_new − log π̃_old)`. Both legs masked with the SAME draw (keyed on stable `(sample_id,position_id)`) → `r_t≈1`, `clipfrac≈0.035` **[FACT both runs]**. Mask corrupts only `∇log π̃`.

**2.2 Biased estimator + delta-method curvature [INFERENCE].** `g_mask = g_true + b + ξ`. Rescale makes ACTIVATIONS unbiased `E[h̃]=h` exactly (keeps `r_t≈1`) **[FACT]**. But the GRADIENT stays biased: `log π̃` is nonlinear in `h̃`, so by 2nd-order expansion `E[F(h̃)] ≈ F(h) + ½ Σ ∂²F/∂h_j² · h_j² · p/(1-p)` — the dropout-as-regularizer curvature term (Wager 2013), nonzero, a force `g_true` lacks. **Unbiased activations ⇏ unbiased gradient.** Variance inflates `p/(1-p)=9` per kept channel ×7 layers **[FACT]**. Class: structured-multiplicative-noise SGD with curvature bias (dropout/DropConnect repurposed as a gradient compressor).

**2.3 Minimal ascent condition [FACT applied].** `E[ΔJ] ≈ η·⟨∇J, E[g_mask]⟩`. Ascent needs only `⟨∇J, E[g_mask]⟩ > 0`, i.e. `‖g_true‖² + ⟨g_true,b⟩ > 0`. Far weaker than "masked forward matches sampler"; the 20M× gap never appears. Variance `ξ` is zero-mean (drops from the 1st-order term), handled by Adam scale-invariance (`m̂/√v̂` cancels constant scaling) + grad-clip — why a 9×-inflated grad-norm doesn't produce a large update, and why the metric is val/score not grad_norm. GRPO ratio-clip is the weight safety-rail; `r_t≈1` keeps the surrogate linear so the projection is meaningful.

**2.4 clean@20 = error-feedback re-sync [INFERENCE].** Bias `b` is systematic (same curvature direction each masked step) → accumulates over a 19-step window (variance averages ∝1/√19, bias doesn't). Unchecked it rotates `g_mask` until projection turns negative. The clean step runs `g_true` exactly and re-anchors first. This is error-feedback compressed SGD (EF21, arXiv 2106.05203; Shi 2108.00951) and Local-SGD periodic full-sync; the K↔speed tradeoff (K=4/5/20 all reach GSM8K parity, larger K slower: steps-to-reward≥0.5 = 17/18/44) is the classic Local-SGD curve. Cleanest account of non-divergence: the RLVR trajectory is near-rank-1 (RELEX 2605.21468, Linear-RLVR 2601.04537) — 19 masked steps are noisy projections of one dominant direction, the clean step re-defines it; masking corrupts magnitude-along-the-line more than rotation-off-it. **[INFERENCE, contingent on rank-1 transferring]**

**2.5 Elicitation vs learning SNR split [INFERENCE, core of Q3].** `b` and `ξ` are mask properties (∝ `p/(1-p)·h²`) → task-independent. `‖g_true‖` is task-dependent. GSM8K: base 0.715, dominant mode, coarse push; `‖g_true‖²≫floor` → positive margin → learns. Big-Math: base 0.48, precise high-dim movement to rare chains, sparse reward → small `‖g_true‖` (even dense barely moves); `‖g_true‖²≲floor` → projection≈0 → stall. Quantified by C4 (Kolomvaki 2602.17423): error floor ∝ mask variance ≈9; GSM8K gain above the floor (reachable), Big-Math below (unreachable). B6 (2605.16787) is the dual gradient-similarity view.

## 3. Empirical evidence (empirical_check.md + verified EXP-20)

**3.1 2×2 (all four cells FACT).** dense GSM8K 0.741; dense Big-Math 0.536→0.608 peak ~0.59 plateau, reward 0.41→~0.58 (**EXP-20 VERIFIED from local train.log**: `comm_eff.enabled=false`, `mask_applications=0` throughout, reached step 102); masked GSM8K 0.085→0.735 ≈ dense −0.8%; masked Big-Math 0.560→0.550 flat. EXP-20 is now FACT (not unverified) — confirms dense learns +0.06 on Big-Math while masked stalls.

**3.2 Sawtooth + precise stationarity [FACT].** masked `pearson≈0.004 / kl≈17 / ppl_ratio≈2.5e7 / entropy≈5.9`; clean `pearson≈0.9995 / kl≈0.0004 / ppl_ratio≈1.0003 / entropy≈0.30–0.41` trending down. Fully resets every clean step (5/5 EXP-17, 4/4 EXP-19). **CORRECTION:** the gap is flat in DIRECTION (pearson slope +4.9e-7, R²≈0) but NOT in absolute level — masked KL creeps +0.006/step, R²=0.98, +0.70 over the run. This is **not** corruption ratcheting: it is the improving true policy widening the gap vs the fixed-variance masked forward (`training_log_ppl` +0.0047/step as `rollout_log_ppl` −0.0012/step). Per-window binning is flat (R²=0.03). Describe as "stationary in direction, absolute level tracks the improving policy," never "flat."

**3.3 P1 within-window reward (direct test of the ascent condition) [FACT].** GSM8K windows (net change / slope / R²): 1–19 +0.030/+0.0009/0.12; 21–39 +0.258/+0.0118/0.85; 41–59 +0.075/+0.0065/0.51; 61–79 +0.040/+0.0035/0.40; 81–99 +0.016/+0.0010/0.09. Big-Math all flat, mean slope +0.0004/step (~10× smaller), R²≈0. GSM8K masked windows carry real learning; Big-Math windows carry none. **Qualification:** GSM8K's FIRST window (1–19) is itself ~flat; the surge is window 2 AFTER the first clean step → the first clean step doesn't just permit masked progress, it INITIATES the learning phase (plausibly by fixing answer format so reward becomes non-degenerate). **[FACT data; INFERENCE format-kickoff]**

**3.4 Clean grad_norm 2× = SNR evidence [FACT].** Repair quality is task-identical, but clean grad_norm EXP-17≈0.40 vs EXP-19≈0.19. This is NOT a contradiction of "identical repair" — it is the direct measurement of `‖g_true‖` (~2× smaller on Big-Math, sparser reward). Repair MECHANISM identical; repair INFORMATION CONTENT not. (Corrects theory.md's earlier "statistically indistinguishable" — true for kl/pearson/ppl_ratio, too strong for grad_norm.)

**3.5 Health [FACT].** True-policy entropy trends down on both (−0.0013/step R²0.84; −0.0015/step R²0.93) — no drift to random. Masked grad_norm bounded, no NaN/Inf. `clipfrac≈0.035 ≪ 0.15` saturation. Masking confined to actor-train (`rollout/ref/val/infer/ckpt=0`; anchor+spectral=0).

## 5. Caveats & open questions

**Alternative hypothesis — memorization not reasoning.** The Spurious Rewards Paradox (Yan et al., 2601.11061) argues Qwen2.5 RLVR gains may be circuit-level memorization retrieval — Functional Anchor L18–20, Structural Adapters L21+. **CONCRETE HOOK:** two of our 7 masked boundaries are **L18 and L21** — exactly that circuit. The GSM8K-tolerance / Big-Math-stall split is ALSO explainable as "retrieval survives a corrupted gradient; competition math has nothing to retrieve." This does NOT invalidate the comms result (val runs the true weights; 0.735 is real), but it means we cannot claim the masked gradient elicits REASONING specifically — retrieval-shortcut elicitation is observationally equivalent here. **Open, not dismissed. [SPECULATION; testable via pass@k k≥128 EXP-17 vs EXP-16, and code-reasoning frequency.]**

**Other.** Elicitation-vs-learning is a spectrum, not binary (B2); the rank-1 mechanism is contingent (test: does more masking enforce more linearity?); `g_mask=g_true+b+ξ` is not directly measurable from logs (bias sign/magnitude is theoretical, supported indirectly); the KL-level-creep wording above; n=1 per task.

**Sharpest open question (decides generalization):** is the Big-Math stall fixable by lowering `p` (more surviving `g_true` → larger SNR), with a threshold `p*` below which it climbs? Theory predicts yes, and predicts GSM8K stays insensitive to `p` at high values. **The p-sweep is the decisive next experiment.**

---

**One-line answer.** The masked actor follows the SAME policy as dense (the unmasked weights); the mask only makes a biased, noisy gradient *estimator* whose 20M× train-inference gap never enters the loss. It learns whenever the estimator keeps a positive projection on the true gradient — which holds with margin on GSM8K (elicitation: base 0.715, a coarse gradient suffices, reward rises within masked windows) and collapses on Big-Math (learning: a small/sparse true gradient buried under the mask's fixed bias+noise floor — dense finds +0.06, masked cannot). `clean@20` is the error-feedback re-sync that keeps the sawtooth from diverging; it repairs identically on both tasks but can only inject the gradient information that exists, which is ~2× smaller on Big-Math.
