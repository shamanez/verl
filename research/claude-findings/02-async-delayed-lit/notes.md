## §0 — The four screening axes (how every row is judged)

Our deployment target is precise and unusual, so it is worth stating the lens before the table:

1. **Space.** Does the correction act on the **gradient**, the **weights**, or the **activation** stream? (Our anchor folds a *gradient* `M = g(θ_{t−K})` into a compressed gradient via a merger.)
2. **Delay model.** **Fixed-vs-variable** and **known-vs-unknown.** Our delay is **variable AND unknown** (a slow anchor serving a fast swarm over a network; staleness fluctuates with congestion).
3. **Objective.** convex / nonconvex-**stationary** / **non-stationary-RL**. Ours is **non-stationary RL**: GRPO regenerates the rollout distribution every step, so the gradient's *distribution* — not just its sample — moves with `θ`.
4. **Cross-rank identity.** Admissible corrections must be derivable from **all-reduced sufficient statistics** so every rank computes the *identical* operator (the shared-`Q`/shared-`M` invariant). A per-rank-fitted correction is inadmissible regardless of how well it converges.

The single most important *transfer* question, recurring per row: **does the convergence guarantee survive when (a) the delay is variable & unknown and (b) the objective is non-stationary?** Most classical results die on (b); several 2025–2026 async-RL papers were written precisely to handle (a)+(b) and are therefore the closest analogues.

## §2 — The fixed-vs-variable-delay axis

Our delay is **variable AND unknown**. Sorting the table on this axis is the fastest way to discard inapplicable methods:

**Assume a FIXED, KNOWN delay (or eliminate delay by version-matching) — the brittle set for us:**
- **Nesterov-async (seed, 2025)** — proves convergence for **fixed delay**; the look-ahead discount `(1−γ)` is tuned to that fixed τ. *This is the project's seed precisely because it is the minimal end of the spectrum — and the variable-delay generalization is the open work.*
- **PipeDream / 2BW** — version-exact stashing = delay *eliminated* by bookkeeping; needs an exact `θ` match we cannot supply.
- **PipeMare** — per-stage delay = pipeline depth, fixed and known.
- **DC-ASGD** — the *correction* `H·Δθ` adapts to the *measured* `Δθ` (so the magnitude is variable), but the method presumes you can read the exact stale-vs-current weight pair; in a swarm with unknown lag you may not know *which* `θ_{t−K}` produced `M`.
- **Basis Rotation (2026)** — delay scales linearly with (fixed) pipeline depth.

**Tolerate VARIABLE / UNKNOWN delay — the admissible set:**
- **Staleness-aware Async-SGD (Zhang 2015)** — LR ∝ 1/τ with τ *observed per gradient*. The original variable-delay handler.
- **SSP (Ho 2013) / Ringmaster (2025)** — bound or **discard** by observed staleness; no fixed-τ assumption. Ringmaster's hard-threshold is the theoretically-optimal version.
- **Mishchenko 2022 / DuDe-SGD 2025 / Tyurin NIGT 2025** — explicitly **arbitrary, unbounded, unknown** delay.
- **VCPO (ESS-LR) / M2PO (2nd-moment) / GAC (bounded but variable)** — the RL-native variable-staleness handlers; **GAC requires only a *bound*, not a known value.**

**What breaks under variable+unknown delay (be explicit):** any correction that is a *fixed function of a single τ* (the seed's `(1−γ)` discount, PipeMare's per-stage schedule) is mis-calibrated the moment the real lag deviates from its design point. The project's two hard constraints — **cross-rank-identical** and **variable-staleness-tolerant** — are therefore *exactly* the two properties that the admissible set has and the brittle set lacks. The correct design move is to take a brittle-set *form* (DC-ASGD's `H·Δθ`, the seed's look-ahead, Basis-Rotation's eigenbasis) and make its *parameters* functions of **all-reduced, slow-varying sufficient statistics** rather than a hardwired τ.

## §3 — The stationary-vs-RL gap (gap-2): per-method survival

**The claim to test, per method:** classical convergence guarantees assume the gradient is a *noisy sample of a fixed* `∇f`. Under GRPO the rollout distribution `d_{π_θ}` moves every step, so `M = g(θ_{t−K})` is a sample of a *different objective* `∇f_{t−K}`, not a stale sample of `∇f_t`. EXP-38 quantifies the consequence: cosine `g(θ_t)·g(θ_{t−K})` decays to **0.18 @K=5, ≈0 @K=10** on GSM8K (near-orthogonal at K=1 on Big-Math). A stationary proof cannot see this because in its world the inner product stays positive by smoothness.

| Method class | Does the guarantee survive gap-2? | Why / why not |
|---|---|---|
| HOGWILD!, Lian 2015, SSP, Local-SGD, SlowMo, Lookahead | **No** | All bound progress via `⟨g(θ_t−τ), ∇f(θ_t)⟩ > 0` under smoothness of a **fixed** `f`. With a moving objective that inner product is exactly what collapses (EXP-38). |
| **Mishchenko 2022 ("delays don't matter")** | **No — and this is the load-bearing rebuttal** | Its optimism is *because* the minimizer is fixed: a stale gradient still points "downhill toward the same `θ*`." Under gap-2 the downhill direction itself rotates each step; the result simply does not apply. **This is the single most important "do not over-claim" guardrail.** |
| Stich-Karimireddy EF (delayed) | **No (as a rate); yes (as a technique)** | The "delay → negligible higher-order term" rests on a fixed objective; under drift the delayed term is *not* asymptotically negligible. But the EF *bookkeeping* (carry the residual) is objective-agnostic and still valid. |
| DC-ASGD, Nesterov-async, PipeMare, AsyncMesh, Basis-Rotation | **Not as proven; the *correction form* may still help** | All proven on stationary LM. Their corrections (`H·Δθ`, look-ahead, eigenbasis rotation) are *geometric* statements about the gradient field that remain *meaningful* under drift — they just lose their convergence certificate. The right reading: **inherit the form, re-validate empirically** (the project's offline cosine-lift gate is exactly this re-validation). |
| **GAC, VCPO, M2PO, MinPRO, IMPALA/V-trace, Tyurin-NIGT** | **Yes — designed for it** | These are the *only* rows whose objective is non-stationary RL with stale rollouts. GAC even *re-derives the staleness convergence guarantee in the RL setting under bounded staleness*, and its empirical "stale-aligned high-cosine → overshoot" is the same phenomenon we see. M2PO names the *budget* ("prosperity before collapse"). **These are the analogues to mine for transferable mechanism.** |

**Net of §3.** The async-RL 2025–2026 cluster is the right home for *guarantees*; the distributed-optimization classics are the right home for *correction forms*. The project's design lives at the intersection: a DC-ASGD/Basis-Rotation-style **curvature** correction (form, from 1B/1C), validated under a GAC/M2PO-style **bounded-staleness trust gate + variance/second-moment control** (guarantee, from 1D), with parameters that are **cross-rank-identical and staleness-adaptive** (admissibility, from §2).

## §4 — Transfer verdict + ranked relevance

### The 4–6 most relevant (mine these for mechanism)

1. **GAC — Gradient Alignment Control (Xu et al. 2026, arXiv:2603.01501).** *The* closest analogue: gradient-space, non-stationary RL, and it independently reports the project's core empirical signature (stale gradients are **abnormally cosine-aligned → overshoot/ignition**) *with* a convergence proof under bounded staleness. Its projection-based damping of the stale-aligned component is the RL-native sibling of the merger. **Read first.**
2. **Basis Rotation (Jung, Shin, Lee, ICML 2026, arXiv:2602.03515).** The external realization of the project's **R5 beyond-diagonal curvature** escape: stale gradients hurt because the **Hessian eigenbasis is misaligned with the coordinate axes**, defeating Adam's diagonal `v_t`; rotating into the eigenbasis is an inherently **off-diagonal, curvature-derived, globally-shared (→ cross-rank-identical)** transform. This is the most credible *surpass*-route precedent in the literature. **Read at full-text depth; it bears directly on the diagonal trap.**
3. **DC-ASGD (Zheng et al. 2017, arXiv:1609.08326).** The exact gradient-space form of "un-rotate via curvature" (`g(θ_t) ≈ g(θ_{t−K}) + H·Δθ`). Adopt the *form*, **but its diagonal Hessian approximation is the diagonal trap incarnate** — the project must go beyond-diagonal (where Basis-Rotation points) to clear parity.
4. **M2PO / "Prosperity before Collapse" (Zhao et al. 2025, arXiv:2510.01161).** The external statement of the staleness *budget*: stale data is informative until, without a trust region, it collapses — EXP-37's stable@5/ignite@10 in words. Its **second-moment IS constraint** is a concrete, cross-rank-computable trust mechanism and overlaps the σ(M) "second-moment/disagreement" route.
5. **VCPO / Stable Asynchrony (Huang et al. 2026, arXiv:2602.17616).** The **ESS-scaled learning rate** is the principled adaptive-dose the project's null EXP-31 sought, and its variance-control framing connects to the σ(M) cross-rank-second-moment route; bonus: it empirically demonstrates the TIS gradient-spike-then-collapse the project calls "ignition."
6. **Nesterov-async, the seed (Ajanthan et al. 2025, arXiv:2505.01099) + AsyncMesh (2026, arXiv:2601.22442).** The baseline rung and its 2026 multi-axis successor; the **fixed-linear look-ahead** is what the project's *learned, variable-staleness, beyond-diagonal* projection must beat, and AsyncMesh's **EMA-on-the-aggregate** correction is a template for the cross-rank-identical slow correction.

### Tempting-but-inapplicable (call out explicitly)

- **Mishchenko 2022 "delays don't matter."** *Stationary-objective optimism.* Do **not** cite as evidence that anchor staleness is benign — gap-2 voids it (§3).
- **PipeDream / 2BW weight stashing.** Needs an **exact** version match → *eliminates* the very staleness we are forced to live with; structurally incompatible.
- **DANA (future-position extrapolation).** Forecasts where `θ` will be = an **anchor-lead** move; forbidden by GOAL.md ("anchor always lags, never leads"). The canonical *inadmissible* form.
- **Lookahead / Lookahead-style optimizer averaging.** Same-data trajectory averaging; no staleness, no drift. Anchor-analogy is superficial.
- **Stich-Karimireddy "delay is negligible" rate.** True only under stationarity; the *technique* (EF) transfers, the *rate* does not.

### One-line synthesis for the lead

> The distributed-optimization literature supplies the **correction forms** (DC-ASGD's `H·Δθ`; the seed's look-ahead; **Basis-Rotation's Hessian-eigenbasis rotation** — the off-diagonal curvature move that escapes the diagonal trap) but every guarantee is **stationary**; the 2025–2026 async-LLM-RL literature supplies the **non-stationary guarantees and the matching empirics** (GAC's stale-aligned-overshoot + projection, M2PO's second-moment trust budget, VCPO's ESS-LR), all under **variable bounded** staleness. The project's contribution sits exactly where these meet and the literature is silent: a **curvature-derived, beyond-diagonal** correction whose parameters are **cross-rank-identical, staleness-adaptive functions of all-reduced statistics**, validated by a **bounded-staleness trust gate** — i.e. lift the form from 1B/1C, lift the guarantee/gate from 1D, and satisfy §2's admissibility.
