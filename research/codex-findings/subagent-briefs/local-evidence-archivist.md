# Local evidence brief: stale anchor gradients in comm-efficient GRPO

Scope: local-only synthesis from `CLAUDE.md`, `CODE_WALKTHROUGH.md`, `runs/EXP-37E`, `runs/EXP-38`, `runs/Q_BASIS_ANALYSIS`, `runs/DELAY_ANALYSIS`, and all report artifacts under `research/reports`. Keep GSM8K and Big-Math separate.

## Orientation from repo docs

- This fork is a research fork for a communication-efficient, pipeline-parallel GRPO trainer on Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct + GSM8K; fixed controls include vanilla GRPO, no KL/no entropy, multi-GPU, and comm-efficient activation masking / anchor / spectral correction work. Source: `/Users/shamane/Documents/verl/CLAUDE.md:11-27`.
- The comm-eff path has two circuits: a fast compressed actor train path and a clean no-optimizer anchor path. The anchor may produce `M` and own `Q`; a merger can rewrite selected fast gradients before `optimizer_step()`. Source: `/Users/shamane/Documents/verl/CODE_WALKTHROUGH.md:9-18`, `/Users/shamane/Documents/verl/CODE_WALKTHROUGH.md:34-47`.
- Invariants that matter for interpretation: anchor passes are unmasked, no optimizer steps, no rollouts/rewards; anchor gradients are DP-mean reduced into `M`; paired replay aligns the anchor with the batch/generator weights that produced the matching fast rollout; probes/captures are telemetry only. Source: `/Users/shamane/Documents/verl/CODE_WALKTHROUGH.md:74-88`.

## Known verdicts for synthesis

- Stale anchor gradient is the primary failure mode: the anchor gradient is a valid on-policy gradient for stale weights/data, then transplanted into current GRPO with no IS correction. It is low-variance but biased toward a defunct policy; under GRPO it becomes a persistent carrier along response-length directions. Source: `/Users/shamane/Documents/verl/research/reports/comm-eff-grpo/why-grpo-fails-sft-works.html:104-121`, `:123-145`, `:163-171`.
- Rollout/train forward mismatch (`rollout_corr`) is not the K=20 collapse cause. It measures vLLM full-rank forward vs actor rank-77 projected forward, is present in both stable and collapsing comm-eff runs, and cannot see stale gradient directions. Source: `/Users/shamane/Documents/verl/research/reports/comm-eff-grpo/rollout-corr-off-policyness.html:65-96`, `:112-128`.
- Q/cadence is secondary, not the core collapse: bootstrap Q is bad but learned Q quickly becomes low-error; K=20 late collapse only mildly raises Q error. Cadence's clearest footprint is a clipfrac spike at every Q refresh. Source: `/Users/shamane/Documents/verl/research/runs/Q_BASIS_ANALYSIS/Q_BASIS_REPORT.md:48-61`, `/Users/shamane/Documents/verl/research/runs/DELAY_ANALYSIS/delay_failure_report.html:228-236`.
- EXP-38 resolves the next-method direction: do not use stale uncompressed gradients as optimizer signal; demote the anchor to slow Q/codec calibration; compress forward activation space with frozen/slow Q; use a separate, higher-rank backward codec; set K/backward rank per task; only forward-codec rank looks globally safe. Source: `/Users/shamane/Documents/verl/research/runs/EXP-38/verdict.md:49-59`, `/Users/shamane/Documents/verl/research/reports/dense-run-behaviour/_joint_narrative.html:30-34`.
- Future extrapolation/projection reports are seeds, not verdicts. They propose a learned or fixed-linear stale-to-live projection and GPU-free EXP-38 offline gate; GSM8K is the feasibility case, Big-Math the stress case; surpass requires genuine off-diagonal/curvature lift, not diagonal rescale. Source: `/Users/shamane/Documents/verl/research/reports/anchor-future-projection/theory-and-literature-2026-06-22.md:9-12`, `:15-51`, `:112-137`, `:188-220`; discussion seed in `/Users/shamane/Documents/verl/research/reports/anchor-future-projection/discussion-2026-06-22.md:8-29`, `:134-156`, `:181-198`.

## Caveats and anti-mixing reminders

- EXP-37 delay/cadence attribution is theory-grounded, not measured, because cadence and delay_K were raised together and share a fire gate. The proposed decoupling 2x2 is still needed. Source: `/Users/shamane/Documents/verl/research/runs/DELAY_ANALYSIS/delay_failure_report.html:228-256`, `/Users/shamane/Documents/verl/research/runs/DELAY_ANALYSIS/cadence_analyst.md:299-325`.
- Q_BASIS did not capture full Q tensors, so it cannot measure Q principal angles/column motion directly; it uses scalar telemetry. Source: `/Users/shamane/Documents/verl/research/runs/Q_BASIS_ANALYSIS/Q_BASIS_REPORT.md:8-11`.
- EXP-38 is n=1 per task and 75 steps; small-lag k<=5 cosines are sampled early; knees are consistency with EXP-37 5/5-vs-20/20, not causal proof; gap 2 is inferred from behavior drift, not directly observed under a live stale anchor. Source: `/Users/shamane/Documents/verl/research/runs/EXP-38/verdict.md:72-73`, `/Users/shamane/Documents/verl/research/reports/dense-run-behaviour/_joint_narrative.html:39-41`.
- Keep GSM8K and Big-Math separate. Do not average tensors, curves, ranks, or staleness budgets. The report folder README and EXP-38 verdict both state dataset-tagged outputs and no tensor/curve merging. Source: `/Users/shamane/Documents/verl/research/reports/dense-run-behaviour/README.md:19-25`, `/Users/shamane/Documents/verl/research/runs/EXP-38/verdict.md:6`.
- Do not generalize GSM8K's marginal 5/5 survival to Big-Math: Big-Math is near-zero gradient cosine at k=1 and has higher gradient/backward ranks. Source: `/Users/shamane/Documents/verl/research/runs/EXP-38/verdict.md:43-51`, `/Users/shamane/Documents/verl/research/reports/dense-run-behaviour/_joint_narrative.html:2-10`.
- Do not mix merger geometries: signed_ema at 20/20 ignites a length spiral; delayed_ef at 20/20 degrades/plateaus without the same length explosion. Same cause family (off-policy staleness), different symptom. Source: `/Users/shamane/Documents/verl/research/reports/comm-eff-grpo/why-grpo-fails-sft-works.html:173-189`, `:269-270`.
- Comm-cost caveat: reported `bytes_ratio ~= 0.0502` counts fast-path Y plus amortized Q; full dense M broadcast is known uncounted. Source: `/Users/shamane/Documents/verl/research/runs/DELAY_ANALYSIS/delay_failure_report.html:290-292`.
