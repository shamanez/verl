VERIFY: CONCERNS

The spectral formula is dimensionally sound for a full thin SVD: `U^T G_mask V` gives a square core, the two-sided reconstruction preserves the original 2D shape, `alpha=1.0` is mathematically a no-op, and `alpha=0` reduces exactly to `G_filt`. The FSDP method is mostly sound because it requires instrumenting `p.grad` representation and testing reduction commutativity before deciding placement. My concern is that the plan still states the correction is applied “after actor backward and before optimizer.step()” while also treating before/after FSDP reduction as open; if FSDP reduction has already happened implicitly during backward, that wording may hide the actual application point unless the logs explicitly prove it. Also, the `(0, 1]` bound for `||G_proj - G_mask|| / ||G_mask||` at `alpha=0.3` is not guaranteed by the formula for arbitrary anchors and gradients; the blend can move farther than `||G_mask||` if `G_filt` is sufficiently different or amplified. That makes the success predicate potentially over-constraining, but it does not invalidate the experiment method.
