The problem

Masking saves inter-stage comm — but the masked gradient won't learn

  • Per-(token,channel) activation masking at the 7 pipeline boundaries (p=0.9) cuts the activation/gradient traffic ~85%.
  • But the masked GRPO gradient is biased & high-variance: reward stalls at the ~0.13 floor — it doesn't learn.
  • A periodic full dense "clean" gradient every K steps does make it learn (EXP-17 reached parity) — proof the signal is recoverable. But it's expensive (full comm every K steps).

M4 — the question

Can a continuous, cheap correction — driven by a STALE (5-step-old) full-gradient reference (the “anchor”) — make the masked curve track dense within ≤50 steps, with clean_cadence OFF?

Hard constraints: delay_K=5 (never fresh — realistic pipeline latency); no periodic clean step; the correction must supply the missing gradient component, not reweight it.

Match metric: mean & final |Δreward| ≤ 0.05 vs the dense curve over steps 1–50.

The load-bearing finding

Two bugs meant the “anchor” never emitted the real gradient

Bug 1 — random-weight clone

Under per-layer FSDP, the anchor's deepcopy fails and the fallback clone has non-infixed param names. Name-matching loaded 0/338 stale weights → the clone ran on random init → garbage G_anchor.

Fix: canonicalize the ._fsdp_wrapped_module infix at every name-key boundary.

Bug 2 — importance-ratio corruption

The anchor reused the batch's masked old_log_probs with an unmasked forward → GRPO ratio ≠ 1 → the PPO clip mangled the gradient. M_anchor was a clip-distorted PPO gradient, not the true gradient.

Fix: a plain policy-gradient anchor loss −(A·logπ·mask)/Σmask (ratio≡1, no clip).

Consequence: prior anchor+spectral “inertness” (EXP-16, val 0.080) was confounded by these bugs — not purely orthogonality. And staleness was never the blocker: once the anchor emits the real delay_K=5 gradient, the method works.

The working method

Clean-stale-anchor blend — replace, don't add

G_corr = (1−η)·G_mask + η·(‖G_mask‖/‖M_anchor‖)·M_anchor
  • M_anchor = a clean plain-policy-gradient (ratio≡1) computed on a delay_K=5-stale weight clone, refreshed every cadence=5 steps — “the clean step's gradient, at stale weights”.
  • Blend, not add: downweight the biased masked gradient and steer toward the true direction, scale-matched so ‖G_corr‖≤‖G_mask‖ (no blow-up).
  • clean_cadence = 0 — the correction stands alone on the stale anchor; no periodic full gradient.
