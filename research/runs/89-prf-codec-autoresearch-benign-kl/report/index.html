Issue #89: Autoresearch, driving the PRF activation codec toward the dropout-benign regime at fixed 95% compression

Final report, 2026-07-23. Run id 89-prf-codec-autoresearch-benign-kl, branch exp/89-prf-codec-autoresearch-benign-kl.

1x H200 SXM, ~41.5 box-hours at $3.97/hr (~$165), 11 cells (3 controls + 8 search trials), zero environment incidents.

Links: GitHub issue #89 · WandB project 89-prf-codec-autoresearch-benign-kl (entity shamanework-pl).

1. Executive summary

The question. Issue #88 closed with PRF-constant (stochastic 95% activation masking, constant 1/(1-p) rescale) as the only clean 5/5 codec, but it left a benign-regime gap: at fixed 95% compression (77 of 1536 hidden values per token), the codec-view entropy reads ~7.8 against the dropout control's ~0.13 (posed in the issue as the 7.84-vs-0.13 gap), and the reference KL (actor/kl_loss) climbs steadily instead of holding a flat dropout-like plateau. #89 ran an autonomous diagnose-and-propose search over one-lever codec variants to close both gaps without breaking within-step ratio cancellation (actor/ppo_kl at machine scale) and without moving the 77-values-per-token byte budget.

The answer: a clean negative under the strict gate, after 8 one-lever candidates. The entropy inflation IS structurally reducible: fast-refresh low-rank residual correction (FRLR) cut codec-view entropy by 63% (7.81 to 2.91) at exactly 77 values per token. But every entropy win re-inflated the reference KL (rms-match 38x, FRLR family 5x to 16x), and the benign corner (low entropy AND incumbent-level KL) was not reachable inside the 40 GPU-hr budget. The recommended config stays PRF-constant plus exact-k, the single gate-legal, within-noise accept.

Mechanism in three lines.

  1. The entropy gap is the constant 20x variance amplification of the rescale, structural rather than stochastic: replace the rescale and entropy drops immediately (rms-match 5.86, FRLR 2.91 to 3.43).
  2. The discarded 95% is not free noise: any lever that reconstructs or rebalances it re-aligns the surviving residual across steps, and coherent residual energy is exactly what the reference KL prices. KL level = residual energy x actor-alignment asymmetry.
  3. The KL slope is set by Q adaptivity, how fast the correction basis tracks the moving actor: fast Q holds KL at 0.003 for 9 steps, slow Q sawtooths between 1.5 and 2.85. Entropy and KL trade through the same residual, so no one-lever fix exists at this horizon.

Metric-geometry caveat. Dropout's entropy, ppo_kl, and kl_loss floors are eval-vs-train VIEW artifacts: dropout perturbs only the training-mode forward, so its rollout and evaluation paths are dense, while every PRF number is computed in the codec view (the trainer's masked forward). Cross-family absolute comparisons (0.137 vs 7.82) therefore compare different measurement geometries; every accept/reject decision in this report is same-geometry, candidate vs incumbent. Likewise rollout_probs_diff_mean ~0.9 on all PRF cells is EXPECTED (the rollout worker is uncompressed; the codec touches only the trainer forward) and was never a stop trigger.

4. Mechanism findings

  1. (i) The entropy inflation is structural (mask geometry), and each alternative source was excluded by a dedicated lever. Not amplification-magnitude alone: rms-match cut entropy only 25% (7.81 to 5.86) at a 38x KL cost. Not count jitter: exact-k is within noise on every axis. Not depth allocation: nonuniform-p moved entropy by 0.01 and worsened KL 14%. Not temporal correlation: antithetic left entropy untouched (+0.0001) and bought only 7% KL.
  2. (ii) FRLR proves 63% structural reduction is possible at exactly 77 values per token. Replacing the 20x rescale with a fast-refresh rank-32 residual correction dropped codec-view entropy 7.81 to 2.91 at the same byte budget, refuting the "amplification is irrelevant" half of the null. The cost lands entirely in the reference KL.
  3. (iii) The KL decomposition. LEVEL = residual energy x actor-alignment asymmetry: levers that re-align the surviving or reconstructed energy across steps (rms-match, all FRLR) pay 5x to 38x on kl_loss. SLOPE = Q adaptivity: fast Q (203 refreshes per 30 steps; 140 by step 21 on r48k28) holds KL at 0.0032 to 0.0034 through step 9 before the climb; slow Q (14 refreshes) shows flat frozen-basis windows (1.43 to 1.52 for steps 2 to 10, 2.48 to 2.85 for 11 to 20) punctuated by anchor-coincident refresh events at steps 11 and 21 (jump 1.52 to 2.48, reset 2.85 to 0.62), the sawtooth in Figure 3.
  4. (iv) The rank ladder is monotone at matched steps, on both the level and the slope of the KL climb. At matched step 21: r16k60 0.210, r32k44 0.125, r48k28 0.051, and the same ordering holds at every matched step past the fast-Q bootstrap. Entropy stays in a narrow 2.9 to 3.7 band across the family, so within the fixed r+k budget, correction rank beats raw kept coordinates; r48k28 dominates the family.
  5. (v) The within-step identity held everywhere: the codec never broke ratio cancellation. actor/ppo_kl stayed below 1e-2 on every candidate at every logged step, with a single transient 0.0109 at step 20 on r16k60; the incumbent reads machine scale (-2.3e-4 at step 40). All damage discussed above is cross-step reference drift, not within-step policy-ratio breakage.

5. Verdict

VERDICT: PASS   A clean, symmetric negative delivered through the plan's own falsifiable branch: the plan pre-declared that if no candidate beats the constant-rescale incumbent under the gate within budget, the shipped trajectory plus the final recommended config is itself the PASS deliverable.

Strict gate outcome. 8 candidates, zero goal hits: 7 outright rejects and 1 within-noise, gate-legal accept (exact-k), so the incumbent codec is unchanged in substance. Every rejection carries a recorded diagnose-and-propose entry; the loop exited only at the money gate. mask_ratio stayed in [0.94, 0.96] on every cell.
