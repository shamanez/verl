Executive summary

There are two policies in this setup. The rollout policy is ordinary vLLM generation and is unmasked. The training/actor policy is the FSDP forward with per-(token,channel) activation masking at p=0.9 + rescale at the pipeline-stage boundaries. The masked actor is therefore a different function from the vLLM sampler that produced the rollouts — that is the importance-sampling (IS) mismatch GRPO must tolerate.

5. What needs to be done (ranked, grounded in measured numbers)

  1. Keep and tune the clean dense step — it is the only lever that recovers accuracy. clean@4 reaches GSM8K val acc 0.696 vs dense 0.741 (within 4.5 pts); cell 7 (clean@5, 50 steps) drives reward from 0.1279 to 0.7637. By contrast anchor@2+spectral@2 with NO clean steps gives val acc 0.080 (≈ base/random) and reward stalls at ~0.13. Action: sweep clean cadence (3/4/5/8) for the accuracy↔comm-cost trade-off.
  2. Anchor+spectral correction, as implemented, does NOT close the train↔inference gap. On the anchor2_spectral2 cell pearson is still ~0.0045 and kl ~16.75 — statistically indistinguishable from the plain masked cells. Action: treat the current anchor/spectral path as ineffective for IS-mismatch reduction; re-derive or re-target it, and always pair with at least one clean step for learnable signal.
  3. To shrink the gap without clean steps, attack the mask directly — measure each against these baselines:
    • (i) Lower p (e.g. 0.5/0.7): less aggressive masking ⇒ higher pearson; trade comm savings for fidelity.
    • (ii) Mask the rollout too / on-policy masked generation so actor == rollout function and the IS mismatch collapses by construction (currently mask_applications/rollout = 0).
    • (iii) Explicit IS-correction term in the GRPO objective targeting the measured kl ~16.8.
  4. No action needed on cross-pass mask correctness or gradient explosion. Mask is bit-consistent across forwards; rescale already fixes the no-rescale grad_norm (~2700 → ~5, a sawtooth bounded by the clean-step resets).
